```text
Create vocal neo-soul at 84 BPM. Record one wholly original six-syllable sung hook. For each syllable, cut the first 80 milliseconds as an onset fragment and separate the remaining voiced vowel tail. Turn only the six onset fragments into the hook's percussion kit through envelope shaping and pitch changes; turn only the six vowel tails into sustained pitched pads through time stretching. Preserve every fragment's original syllable order and onset position.

The verses may use a dry original lead voice, bass, and drums, but each chorus must mute the intact hook recording completely and reconstruct it only from its onset-percussion and vowel-pad halves. Do not add unrelated percussion or pads during the reconstructed hook. All lyrics, melody, harmony, singing, source audio, and production must be original. Do not imitate DJ HASEBE, the referenced song, its vocalist, recognizable phrasing, riffs, recording, or samples.
```
