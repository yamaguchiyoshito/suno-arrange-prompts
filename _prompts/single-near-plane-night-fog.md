```text
Create a slow vocal trip-hop piece using mabanua's “Night Fog” only as a high-level point of departure. Write independent parts for one lead voice, electric piano, and synth bass, then assign them to exactly three depth planes: near is dry and full-band; middle uses a 0.8-second room and a 6 kHz low-pass; far uses a 3.2-second dark hall and a 2 kHz low-pass. Every eight bars rotate the instruments near to middle, middle to far, and far to near. Keep exactly one layer in each plane and exactly one layer dry at all times. Notes, rhythm, dynamics, and stereo positions must not change during a rotation. Drums remain quiet, mono, and fixed in the middle plane. Complete three rotations so every layer visits every depth once, then end on the next dry vocal breath. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate mabanua or the source track.
```
