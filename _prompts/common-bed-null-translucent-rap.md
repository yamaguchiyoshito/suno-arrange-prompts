```text
Create two original sixteen-bar versions at 90 BPM from an exactly identical mono drum-and-bass bed. Version A adds one dry original rap take; version B replaces the rap with an original flute line while leaving the common bed sample-accurately unchanged. Present A, then B. For the final section, sum A with a polarity-inverted copy of B at equal gain so the shared drums and bass cancel, leaving only the difference between rap and flute. Do not compress, pan, automate, or process either common bed differently, and do not add material after the null operation. Verify the final section has no audible kick or bass. End on the remaining flute decay. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
