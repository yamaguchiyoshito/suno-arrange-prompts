```text
Create an 80 BPM vocal abstract hip-hop loop with four envelope-controlled roles: wooden hit, kick, electric-key note, and metallic tap. Write one fixed four-bar pattern with at least two events per role and varied gaps. For every event, measure the time to the next event of the same role, including the wrap to the loop start. Set decay to exactly half that gap, clipped to 120–960 milliseconds. Recalculate per event; never assign decay by instrument. Preserve attacks, pitches, positions, velocities, panning, rhythm, and event count. Use no reverb, delay, or substitute samples. Keep a dry original voice and quiet mono bass pulse unchanged for four loops, remove the voice for one final loop, then stop. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
