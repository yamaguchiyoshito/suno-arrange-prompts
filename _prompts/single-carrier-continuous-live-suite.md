```text
Create a continuous four-scene live trance-rock suite for lead vocal, drums, bass, organ, and electric guitar. Give each scene exactly 16 bars and a distinct original harmony, groove, and vocal register, but insert no silence between scenes.

At boundary one, bass alone must sustain and keep pulsing across one full transition bar while voice, drums, organ, and guitar stop. At boundary two, organ alone crosses in the same way. At boundary three, guitar alone crosses. The designated carrier may gradually alter one pitch or rhythm to introduce the next scene; all other players must re-enter together on beat 1 after the transition bar. Never use two carriers, a crossfade, backing track, or ambient pad to conceal a boundary. End scene four with the entire band stopping together after exactly 67 total bars. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
