```text
Create a 90 BPM instrumental hip-hop track from five original percussive strikes recorded separately in one real room. For each recording, isolate the direct first 50 ms and the first clearly audible room reflection, preserving its measured delay. In the first four bars play only the five direct pieces as a beat; in the next four bars mute them and play only their matching reflections at the original absolute offsets. Alternate direct and reflected blocks four times. Do not use digital delay, copied echoes, time-stretching, or artificial reverb. Use only original recordings, rhythm, and harmony; avoid recognizable samples.
```
