```text
Create a humid 75 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop piece with a stumbling dry break, elastic sub-bass, wet electric-piano shards, and clipped saxophone fragments. Write one original four-eighth-note jazz fragment and present it once every four bars. On successive appearances, remove its ending one eighth note at a time: lengths must be 4, then 3, then 2, then 1 eighth note.

Do not compress or refill the removed duration; preserve it as black silence. The fifth scheduled appearance must be completely silent while drums and bass continue unchanged. Repeat the five-stage process with a new fragment, alternating piano and saxophone as the carrier. Never restore a shortened fragment. End at the second silent fifth stage. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
