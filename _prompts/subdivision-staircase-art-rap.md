Create fast vocal abstract hip-hop at 132 BPM in constant 4/4 with a fixed kick-snare frame, centered synth bass, clipped electric piano, and agile original rap. In consecutive bars, divide the full bar evenly into three, four, five, six, then seven parts. Place one short piano attack at every division. Only one subdivision may exist in a bar; do not layer tuplets or alter the drums.

After the seven-part bar, insert one complete 4/4 pivot bar. Keep the fixed drums unchanged; voice, piano, and bass strike only with the existing beat-one kick, creating one unison accent without adding a drum hit. Then descend through seven, six, five, four, and three divisions, one bar each. Keep pitch set, velocity, tempo, and drum pattern fixed. End after the final three-part bar. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
