```text
Create a solo prepared-piano piece treating one instrument as a percussion ensemble. Divide the keyboard into low wooden thuds, middle metallic rattles, and high dry piano tones. Write four short rhythmic cells of unequal length and preserve them exactly. Build sections by changing only order, register, and spacing; never turn pitches into melody or harmony. Begin with one low cell separated by long silences. Add the middle cell as an answer, then let the high cell interrupt irregularly. In the center, overlap all four cells once with crisp attacks and no pedal. Then remove one zone at a time until one muted low strike remains. Preserve mechanical noise and uneven decay. Avoid vocals, chords, flowing arpeggios, steady groove, cinematic crescendos, and added instruments.
```
