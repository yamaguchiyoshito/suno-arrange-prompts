```text
Acoustic-led with capo 2, open chords sustaining. Strum steady 8ths with ghost strokes for drive.

Verse: single acoustic + light tambourine. Pre-chorus adds bass (root-focused, minimal passing tones).
Chorus brings drums (steady kick, slightly laid-back snare) and electric guitars—clean to light drive—for fills and width.
Add subtle string pads only late in the chorus.

Vocal is close and centered; light compression, short plate.
Double very lightly in chorus; harmonies (3rds) only at key moments.

Mix: midrange-focused (clean 200–2 kHz), tight lows. Acoustics panned wide, vocal forward. Keep reverb restrained for clarity.
Build by layering, not loudness; keep the delivery direct and unforced.
```
