Create an original 90 BPM rap arrangement around a 4-by-4 sampler pad grid. Number the pads left to right, top to bottom, and trigger them only in this repeating two-bar path: 1, 2, 6, 5, 9, 13, 14, 10, 11, 15, 16, 12, 8, 7, 3, 4. Assign original kick, snare, hat, bass, chord, and noise hits across the sixteen pads, with every pad used once per path. Build four sixteen-bar sections. Keep the cyclic path unchanged, but begin the accent cycle at pads 1, 6, 11, and 16 respectively; accent every fourth visited pad. Place concise rap verses above the beat without changing the pad route. Avoid fills, extra percussion, tempo changes, and quantization drift. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
