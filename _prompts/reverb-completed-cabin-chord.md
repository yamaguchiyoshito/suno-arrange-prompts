```text
Create intimate free-time acoustic folk with close lead vocal and dry fingerpicked nylon-string guitar. During every sung phrase, let the guitar state only the root and fifth of each new chord; never play its third.

After the singer releases the last syllable, generate a soft pitched vocal-reverb tail that supplies the missing third. Do not change harmony until that tail becomes audible, so the room appears to complete the chord. Alternate minor and major thirds for emotional motion while keeping the guitar figure spare. Introduce a muted low drum only after three completed chords, then remove it before the final verse. End with one unresolved root-and-fifth whose missing third never arrives. Compose wholly original music and words; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, chord signature, vocal delivery, recording, or sample.
```
