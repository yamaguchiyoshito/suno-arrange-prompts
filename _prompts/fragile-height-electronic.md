```text
Start with a fragile music-box synth, soft sub-bass pulses, and distant field-recording ambience.
Keep the verse intimate and restrained, with dry close vocals over minimal electronic clicks.
Gradually introduce warm strings, broken beat drums, and glacial pads that widen the stereo field.
The chorus should feel like emotional release: bigger drums, rising string ostinatos, shimmering synth arps, and deep bass movement.
Maintain a contrast between domestic quiet and dangerous height.
End by stripping everything back to voice, low drone, and a fading metallic bell.
```
