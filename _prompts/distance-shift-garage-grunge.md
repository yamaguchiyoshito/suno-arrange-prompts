```text
Create raw one-take garage grunge around a short, newly written guitar riff. Keep its notes, rhythm, gain, and picking force identical in every section. Put distorted guitar, blunt bass, live drums, and an urgent original vocal in one untreated room with no doubles. Begin with distant miking so reflections blur attacks. At each section boundary, move closer without changing the performance. Let middle distance reveal snare crack and bass definition; let the nearest position expose pick noise, breath, cymbal splash, amplifier hiss, and preamp saturation. Hold master level steady so density comes only from proximity and direct-to-room ratio. Avoid added instruments, tempo ramps, extra distortion, transition fills, vocal tuning, borrowed riffs, and a quiet bridge. Cut abruptly during the closest riff instead of resolving or fading.
```
