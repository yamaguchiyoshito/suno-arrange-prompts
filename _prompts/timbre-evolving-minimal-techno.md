```text
Create instrumental minimal techno from a rigid four-on-the-floor kick, a clipped one-note bass pattern, a dry closed hat, and one short synth stab. Fix every pitch, onset, and accent outside the break below. Change exactly one sound parameter every eight bars: first filter cutoff, then envelope decay, saturation, stereo width, and room size. Move each parameter in small steps and leave all others unchanged. After forty bars, reset one parameter at a time in reverse order while the sequence continues. For the sole break, mute every non-kick track for four bars without moving its programmed events; add no fill. Keep the low end centered and let only the stab widen. Avoid melody, chord progression, vocals, pads, risers, crashes, solos, and a festival drop. End by closing the bass filter over the unchanged final loop, then cut on beat one.
```
