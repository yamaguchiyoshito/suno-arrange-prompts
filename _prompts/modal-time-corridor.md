```text
Modal center shifts between natural minor and Dorian to keep ambiguity.
Lead: airy synth/flute, light portamento and short mordents. Accompaniment: 16th-note arpeggios using add9 and quartal stacks; avoid fixed cadences.

Rhythm: minimal or no kick. Low end: soft drone + restrained bass for float. Pads: slow attack layers; gently sweep filters to imply time.

Form: 8-bar cells with micro-variation—add layers, octave doubles, sparse countermelody. No big chorus; keep midrange clear and transparent. Mix: hall + plate, longer pre-delay; dotted-8th delay tucked into tails. End on suspended/slash harmony, no full resolution.
```
