```text
Create a chorusless abstract hip-hop suite crossing four sonic rooms. Use original rap with changing cadence and no repeated hook. Place one fixed three-hit marker at every threshold while everything around it changes. Room one pairs dry drum-machine gaps with low synth-bass answers. Hard-cut to room two: remove both and place voice over a blurred analog pad and isolated rim clicks. Room three fractures the pulse into uneven bursts and silence, doubling only final consonants. Room four discards prior accompaniment for close voice, low room resonances, and distant noise flashes. Keep each room harmonically narrow; splice immediately and never restore a retired sound. Avoid verse-chorus form, sung refrain, sample quotation, orchestral lift, and recap. Finish when the marker announces a fifth room that never arrives.
```
