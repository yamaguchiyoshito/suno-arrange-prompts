Sparse acoustic arrangement centered on Rhodes piano and acoustic guitar, with acoustic bass, minimal drums, and a restrained lonesome horn.

Rhodes carries the harmony with broken chords, light high-note ornaments, and space. Avoid heavy left hand; let acoustic bass own the low end. Acoustic guitar repeats a simple dotted-8th motif with light reverb, panned wide, answering the piano.

Drums enter late with brushes: soft kick, dry snare, restrained cymbals.
Form: quiet → slight swell → quiet. Use maj7/add9/6/9 colors, avoid strong cadences.
