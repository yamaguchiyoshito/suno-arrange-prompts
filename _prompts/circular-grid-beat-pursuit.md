```text
Use a circular sixteen-step bar at 100 BPM. In round one, a low kick leader starts at step 1 and a dry rim follower starts at step 10. After every bar, move the leader one step clockwise, then move the follower two steps along the shortest circular route toward the leader; if both routes tie, move clockwise. Play one leader and one follower attack per bar at their current positions. They must meet on bar eight, where a single layered impact replaces the two attacks. In round two, reset them to steps 16 and 7 and mirror all movement counterclockwise for another eight-bar pursuit. Keep a one-note bass pulse on each downbeat and add nothing else. End on the second collision. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
