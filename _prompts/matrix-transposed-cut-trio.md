```text
Create an original instrumental cut-up trio at 120 BPM using dry electric guitar G, bass B, and drums D. Treat each row of this 4x4 carrier matrix as one 4/4 bar, with columns as beats: G . B D / . D G . / B G . D / D . B G. A letter permits exactly one short attack by that instrument; a dot is total silence including tails. Bars 1–4 read the four rows from left to right. Bars 5–8 read the transposed matrix in the same way, turning the original columns into rows. Repeat the eight-bar form three times without fills, pickup notes, overlaps, quantization changes, or added layers. Give each instrument one fixed pitch or voicing, timbre, duration, gain, and pan so only matrix transposition changes the sequence. Stop at the final cell with no fade. Use wholly original musical material; do not reuse any existing melody, signature riff, performance, recording, or sample, and do not imitate the source artists.
```
