```text
Create a 68 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop piece with a slow halftime drum machine, deep held sub notes, a huge wordless synthetic vowel cloud, and rare high glass-like flashes. Put the main snare on beat 3 of every bar. The vowel cloud may swell across the bar, but both its dry signal and reverb send must fall to absolute silence for the final eighth note immediately before every snare.

Bring the cloud back only one sixteenth note after the snare, never on the hit. Keep kick, sub, and high flashes unchanged so this repeated pre-snare vacuum is the central motion. Every eighth bar, extend the gate to two eighth notes, then return to one. Finish with the cloud cut and the last snare ringing alone. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
