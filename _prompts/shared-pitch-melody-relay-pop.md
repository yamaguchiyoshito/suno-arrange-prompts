```text
Write clear indie pop at 94 BPM with bass and light drums supporting a melodic relay among lead voice, piano, organ, and clean guitar. Compose one original chain of eight principal pitches and divide it into four consecutive two-note cells.

Voice performs cell 1, piano cell 2, organ cell 3, and guitar cell 4. At each handoff, make the outgoing cell’s second pitch identical to the incoming cell’s first pitch and sustain both timbres together for exactly one beat. Outside those three shared notes, only the assigned carrier may state the chain. Repeat the relay four times, transposing the entire chain while preserving carrier order and rhythm. On the last pass, reverse the carriers to guitar, organ, piano, then voice, and let the final voice note end dry. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
