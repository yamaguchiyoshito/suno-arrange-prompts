```text
Write an original 96 BPM rap using four clearly spoken cue words created for this piece: low, cut, hold, and turn. Place exactly one cue on beat four of every odd-numbered bar. Execute it once on beat one of the following even bar: low triggers a sub-bass note, cut creates one full beat of silence, hold sustains the current chord for one extra beat, and turn reverses only the order of the next four percussion hits. No action may occur in the cue bar, and no action may persist beyond its single response bar. Use the cue order low-cut-hold-turn twice, then the reverse order once. Keep the underlying drum pattern and tempo fixed outside each commanded action. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
