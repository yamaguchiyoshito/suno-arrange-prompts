Create gentle glitch pop at 118 BPM with a close original vocal, muted electric piano, soft synth bass, and clipped broken beat. Establish one calm eight-bar phrase with a fixed event count. Every four bars, replace exactly one sung or instrumental event with a digital burst matching its onset, duration, loudness, and stereo position. The source must disappear; never layer burst and source. Cycle through voice, keyboard, bass, and percussion so no family loses two events in succession. At half replacement, hold once, then restore every source in reverse order. Preserve harmony, tempo, phrase length, and density. Avoid random stutters, fills, drops, risers, pitch spirals, and harsh mastering. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
