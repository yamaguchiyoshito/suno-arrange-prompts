```text
Create patient long-form dub around one melodic bass cycle and slow, laid-back drums. Hold harmonic loop; shape movement through subtraction, space, and timed returns, not chord changes or verse/chorus.

Start with bass, restrained kick, rim-click, faint organ. Once settled, add clean guitar echo, sparse keys, and calm lead vocal with long instrumental gaps. Apply spring reverb, tape-delay, filter sweeps, and feedback tails to individual notes, never whole mix.

Use 16/32-bar cycles. Each removes a major element: drums, harmony, then all but bass and delay residue. Let each sparse state settle; rebuild singly in altered registers or echo timings. Never fill gaps, rush transitions, or use festival drops. Save full ensemble for a final rise: stronger playing, denser echoes, overlapping returns over unchanged bass. Then remove drums and upper parts. End unresolved with bass motif, distant vocal trace, and self-oscillating delay.
```
