```text
Create vocal loop-driven math rock with tight swing. Lock one new guitar figure, dry live drums, and a low-guitar line into the same repeating bar. Keep notes and accents stable; no fill may lengthen the form.

Use one new neutral syllable as the vocal hook. Hold its pitch and rhythm while its formant moves through four steps every eight bars: small and bright, natural, broad, then low and hollow. Mark each change with a half-beat ensemble stop, then resume in unison. Build intensity only through drum weight, octave doubling, and wider guitar placement. Add no bridge, solo, chord change, or chorus. After step four, remove guitar layers one by one as the voice returns to natural tone; end with one dry drum hit. Never recreate an existing melody, lyric, signature riff, vocal identity, recording, or sample.
```
