Create an instrumental abstract hip-hop beat using MASS-HOLE's “santoku knife” only as a high-level point of departure. Record one original sustained chord, cut its sounding duration into three contiguous slices with lengths in the exact ratio 3:2:1, and label them A, B, and C. Build six four-bar sections, assigning one permutation to each section in this order: ABC, ACB, BAC, BCA, CAB, CBA. Repeat that section's ordering as needed, but each three-slice statement must use every slice exactly once. Do not stretch, reverse, overlap, retune, crossfade, or duplicate a slice. Keep one dry, slow drum pattern unchanged beneath all six sections. After CBA, play A, B, and C simultaneously once at their original start point, cut them to the shortest slice length, and stop. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate MASS-HOLE or the source track.
