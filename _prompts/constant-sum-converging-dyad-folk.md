```text
Create a warm 72 BPM two-voice folk piece with acoustic guitar and restrained hand percussion. Write one original eight-bar vocal phrase containing exactly three sustained dyad events, opening at intervals of 12, 8, and 4 semitones in that order. After each full pass, move the lower note of every dyad up one semitone and its upper note down one semitone. This preserves each dyad's pitch sum and midpoint while shrinking its interval by two semitones. Once a dyad reaches unison, keep it there while the wider events continue converging. Perform the opening state followed by six updated repeats: seven passes total. Preserve lyrics, rhythm, note lengths, event order, guitar voicings, percussion, dynamics, and room sound throughout. Sing the all-unison seventh pass completely, then stop without a resolving chord. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and sounds; do not imitate any identifiable song, arrangement, or vocal likeness.
```
