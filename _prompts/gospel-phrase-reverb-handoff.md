```text
Create gospel-inflected ambient around 54 BPM without drums. A close solo voice sings one original phrase of three to six words, then stays silent as long reverb carries its final vowel. Begin the next phrase only after the prior tail becomes inaudible; its disappearance cues the next inhale. Alternate short and long phrases without overlapping dry voices. A soft organ enters only in each tail's final third, holds one chord, and ends before the next voice. After four phrases, add one distant wordless response in the tail only. Increase decay from four to twelve seconds while removing one organ note per phrase. Avoid bass, synced delay, melisma, stacked choir, chorus form, and climax. End when the last tail disappears. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
