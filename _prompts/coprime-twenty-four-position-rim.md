Create a 24-bar vocal neo-soul rap at 80 BPM. Divide every 4/4 bar into twenty-four equal slots, six per quarter note. Add exactly one dry rim accent to each bar. With bars numbered n=0 through 23, place that accent at slot p=(5n mod 24). Because 5 and 24 are coprime, the twenty-four bars must visit every slot exactly once before ending. Do not add another rim, flam, drag, or fill.

Keep an understated kick-snare pocket, warm electric-piano chords, and round synth bass stable beneath the traveling marker. Write original rap verses across bars 1-16 and an original sung refrain across bars 17-24, without changing the rim formula or resetting it at the vocal boundary. Stop after bar 24 before the accent can return to slot 0. Use wholly original melody, harmony, lyrics, performances, recordings, and sounds. Do not imitate Sweet William, Tsubaki, the referenced track, recognizable flows, riffs, recording, or samples.
