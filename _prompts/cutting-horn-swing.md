```text
Create a fast vocal swing-jazz arrangement powered by walking upright bass, crisp ride, light snare comping, and sharp piano chords. Begin with four bars of bass and piano before the full pulse enters. Keep the lead vocal agile, leaving one- or two-beat gaps at phrase endings. Fill only those gaps with compact trumpet-and-sax replies of two to four notes; never sustain horns beneath the voice. Alternate vocal statements and horn answers throughout. At midpoint, trade two-bar passages among piano, horns, and voice over bass and drums. Use one stop-time hit to launch the final section, then restore the walking line immediately. Let the last vocal phrase finish alone, followed by one dry unison horn chord. Avoid strings, synth pads, rock backbeats, long solos, and big-band stacks.
```
