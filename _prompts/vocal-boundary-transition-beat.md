```text
Write an original vocal track at 84 BPM and mark each sixteenth-note slot as voice present or voice absent. Accompaniment may occur only when that binary state changes. At every 0-to-1 transition, play one short low drum and one bass attack; at every 1-to-0 transition, play one dry electric-piano chord and one rimshot. Play nothing at 0-to-0 or 1-to-1 positions. Treat gaps shorter than one sixteenth as continuous voice so they create no false boundary. Repeat the same vocal rhythm twice with different original words, forcing the same accompaniment map, then end with the boundary-triggered instrumental map alone. No pad, sustained bass, pickup, fill, or background percussion may cross a boundary. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
