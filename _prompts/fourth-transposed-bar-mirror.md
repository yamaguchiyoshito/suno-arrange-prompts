Do not add fills, pickups, cymbal tails, or automation that crosses the center bar. Repeat the nine-bar form three times without variation; on the third pass, omit the central chord so bar 5 becomes total silence, while both mirrored halves remain unchanged. End precisely after the final mirrored event. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate Nujabes or the source track.
