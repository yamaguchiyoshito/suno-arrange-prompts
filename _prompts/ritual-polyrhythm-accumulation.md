```text
Create ritual polyrhythm with taiko, struck metal, and low male chant. Begin with one deep drum cycle and no harmony. Add a second drum on another cycle, then hand drums, bells, and metal on further cycles. Keep patterns stable so their meeting points shift while one pulse stays clear.

Use short non-lexical syllables, first in low unison, then as antiphonal groups entering at different points. No verse, melodic chorus, or soloist. Raise intensity only through more performers, octave doublings, and tighter responses; keep tempo and tonal center fixed.

Avoid rock backbeat, strings, chord changes, synth risers, and cinematic hits. Preserve raw room reflections and distance between drum groups. Sustain the densest interlock without harmonic climax. Remove voices, then metal; repeat the first deep-drum cycle once and stop abruptly.
```
