```text
Create an instrumental neo-soul arrangement using mabanua's “Overlap” only as a high-level point of departure. Alternate two newly composed four-bar phrases: phrase A belongs to electric guitar and phrase B to electric piano. At their first boundary, B must begin one eighth note before A ends. Increase that boundary overlap by exactly one eighth note on each later transition: two, three, then four eighth notes. Whenever overlap grows, remove the same number of attacks from the middle two bars of the outgoing phrase, keeping the total number of guitar-plus-piano attacks constant across every eight-bar cycle. Drums, bass, harmony, velocity, and tempo remain fixed. After the four-eighth overlap, reverse the process one step per boundary until the phrases only touch without overlap, then stop. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate mabanua or the source track.
```
