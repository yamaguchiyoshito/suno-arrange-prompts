Create an 80 BPM instrumental neo-soul hip-hop piece with electric piano, bass guitar, and brushed drums. Record four separate eight-bar ensemble takes. Begin each take with one audible original inhale and end it with one exhale; between those breaths the performance must remain continuous, with no punch-ins, copied bars, quantization, or repair edits. Join the four takes only after each exhale, preserving their small tempo and touch differences. Do not turn the breaths into a metronome or percussion loop. Write and perform all harmony and rhythm from scratch; avoid recognizable melodies, samples, or artist imitation.
