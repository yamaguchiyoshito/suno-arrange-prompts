```text
Create instrumental spiritual jazz around continuous tanpura drone, modal upright-bass ostinato, harp, hand percussion, and airy soprano saxophone. Use no chord progression. Begin with drone and a four-note bass cycle; place unequal harp fragments across the pulse. Gradually add brushed cymbal, small bells, and low hand drum, changing density without tempo. Bring saxophone in late with long phrases that begin after the bass downbeat and end before the next harp figure. Give every instrument a different phrase length so alignments drift. Reach the peak through closer entrances and wider sax register, not louder drums. Remove sax, percussion, and bass in that order. Leave harp harmonics over drone and end without cadence. Avoid piano, electronic pads, walking bass, swing comping, and sectional chord changes.
```
