```text
Create an instrumental hardware breakbeat at 108 BPM from one original eight-bar drum, bass, and sampler cycle. Repeat it unchanged at first. On the final bar of cycle two, move one exposed snare exactly one sixteenth note early; this is the only initiating error. In cycle three, keep that snare displaced and move the nearest bass attack to the same early grid point. In cycle four, preserve both changes and move one sampler stab to that point. Do not correct, average, or duplicate any displaced event. Keep every other note, rest, sound, level, and tempo fixed so the accumulated timing mutation is audible. After all three parts inherit the error, repeat the altered cycle twice and stop on its last displaced hit. Avoid fills, risers, chord changes, swing, random glitches, and extra layers. Use entirely original material; do not imitate or reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature rhythm, recording, or sample.
```
