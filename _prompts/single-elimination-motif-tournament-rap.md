```text
Compose eight original one-bar horn motifs M1-M8 at 98 BPM, each with exactly four notes and a preset distinct RMS level. Stage a single-elimination tournament: pair 1v2, 3v4, 5v6, and 7v8, then pair the winners, then the final two. In every four-bar match, alternate the paired motifs for three bars and play the louder preset motif in bar four. That winner advances while permanently absorbing only the highest note of the loser as a fifth note; all other rhythm and pitch material stays unchanged. If levels tie, the lower-numbered motif wins. Keep drums, bass, and an original rap refrain constant. The champion plays alone for eight bars with its three inherited notes, then stop. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
