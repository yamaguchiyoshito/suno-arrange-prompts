```text
Build a delicate 78 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop miniature from soft broken percussion, tiny mono sub notes, submerged chord fragments, and exactly eight newly created object sounds such as taps, grains, paper ticks, and ceramic knocks. Each object sound may occur exactly twice in the whole track.

Place its first occurrence at a unique time hard left; place an identical second occurrence exactly two bars later hard right. Preserve pitch, duration, envelope, and level between the pair. After the right-side twin, never reuse, reverse, stretch, echo, or derive another sound from that object. Introduce one new pair every two bars while the drums remain centered and sparse. End when the eighth right-side twin decays. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
