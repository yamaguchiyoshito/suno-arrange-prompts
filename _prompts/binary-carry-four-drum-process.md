```text
Create a 120 BPM instrumental process-minimal piece with four distinct acoustic drum voices: low kick, dry snare, tuned tom, and metal plate. Treat each bar as a counter moving through binary states 0000 to 1111 over sixteen equal steps. Assign one bit to each drum. At every transition, strike a drum only when its bit changes from the preceding state; compare the first state with the previous bar's 1111. All four drums therefore strike together twice per cycle: at 0111→1000 and at the 1111→0000 bar reset. Repeat this exact event logic for 32 complete bars. Add no extra hits, fills, flams, rolls, ghost notes, or quantization drift. A quiet sustained bass tone and narrow noise bed may change color but add no pulse. After state 1111 ends bar 32, play one final reset unison at the next downbeat and stop immediately. Use wholly original sounds and recordings; do not imitate any identifiable composition, rhythm, production, or performance.
```
