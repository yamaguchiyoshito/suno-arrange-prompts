```text
Create sun-warmed vocal dub at 80 BPM with rounded electric bass, light drums, clean guitar, sparse piano, and relaxed lead voice. Limit bass notes to exactly three durations: an eighth note, a quarter note, or a whole note.

After every eighth-note bass attack, guitar may answer once before the next bass note while piano stays silent. After every quarter-note bass attack, piano may answer once while guitar stays silent. During every whole-note bass sustain, both guitar and piano must remain completely silent, leaving only bass, drums, and voice. Write an eight-bar bass cycle containing four short, four medium, and two long notes, then repeat it without changing their order. On each repeat, use new answer pitches but preserve all gates and durations. End on a whole bass note with no upper reply. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
