```text
Create an 84 BPM instrumental minimal hip-hop track whose entire accompaniment comes from one original ten-second recording of white noise. Derive the kick with a steep low-pass envelope, the snare with a short mid-band burst, hats with high-pass slices, bass with a resonant tracked filter, and chords with four tuned narrow bands. Do not use any oscillator, conventional drum sample, pitched recording, or additional noise file. Preserve a faint unfiltered trace of the source beneath all sections so their common origin remains audible. All composition and source audio must be original; do not imitate an existing recording or mix.
```
