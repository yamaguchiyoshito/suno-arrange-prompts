```text
Create vocal abstract hip-hop at 88 BPM with a fixed two-bar drum pattern, steady sub bass, sparse electric-piano chords, and low-key rap. Treat drum velocity as a budget of sixteen units shared by kick, snare, closed hi-hat, and rim. Begin with allocations of 6, 6, 2, and 2 units respectively. Keep every hit position and the number of hits fixed.

Every eight bars, transfer one unit from kick to hat and one from snare to rim, without changing the total. Repeat four transfers to reach 2, 2, 6, and 6 units, then reverse the transfers in the same order. Keep bass, harmony, voice, and master level stable; use no fills or added layers. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
