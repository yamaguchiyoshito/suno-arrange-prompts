```text
Create an 88 BPM instrumental hip-hop track from one fully arranged original four-bar loop. Duplicate it into six blocks. In every later block, destructively erase exactly two new sixteenth-note cells from the bounced audio; never add, move, repitch, reverse, or replace a sound. Every erased cell remains silent in all following blocks, and cuts must be instantaneous with no fade or reverb fill. Choose the deletions so the groove remains recognizable until the last block, then end without restoring the source loop. Use only self-created music and recordings; do not sample or imitate any existing work.
```
