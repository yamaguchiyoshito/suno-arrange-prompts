```text
Create original vocal shoegaze at 86 BPM with four distinct distorted guitars, steady bass, dry drums, and restrained lead voice. Give guitars G1–G4 fixed phrases, registers, tones, distortion, pans, and durations. In the first eight bars set their gains to -6, -12, -18, and -24 dB respectively. Every eight bars, rotate only the four gain values so the -6 dB lead rank passes G1 to G2 to G3 to G4; after thirty-two bars every guitar has occupied every rank exactly once. Keep bass, drums, voice, effects, and master level unchanged. Do not add layers, automate filters, move pans, rewrite notes, or compensate quieter guitars. Cut after G4 completes its -6 dB block, before the opening ranking returns. Use wholly original music and lyrics; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, sample, guitar identity, or vocal likeness, and do not imitate the source artists.
```
