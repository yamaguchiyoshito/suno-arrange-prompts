```text
Create a light instrumental hip-hop beat using Nujabes' “Feather” only as a high-level point of departure. Write one original six-attack piano motif and keep its pitches, onsets, and durations fixed. Map the Fibonacci weights 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 to velocities 44, 44, 52, 62, 78, and 102. Every two bars rotate that six-value velocity row one attack to the right while the notes stay in place. Complete six rotations so every note receives every weight once, then return to the initial mapping. A soft drum loop and bass line may continue, but neither may accent the current loudest piano note or change across rotations. After the restored mapping plays once, end on the quiet second attack rather than the strongest one. Use only newly created material: every sound, melody, lyric, performance, and recording must be original; do not imitate Nujabes or the source track, and do not reuse its riffs, samples, or vocal phrasing.
```
