```text
Build fast noise-punk from seven original one- or two-bar modules. Use dry drums, distorted bass, and pitch-shifted guitar chirps, scrapes, and stutters. Give each module one fixed rhythm and register; never develop it internally. Switch modules through full-band dead stops lasting one eighth note, one quarter note, or one complete bar. Use newly written single-word shouts and non-lexical bursts as punctuation, not a continuous lyric or melodic chorus. Repeat the module order once, then reverse three adjacent modules while preserving every stop length. Keep bass locked to kick so the cuts remain readable. Avoid sustained chords, ambience, breakdown builds, guitar solos, cymbal washes, and gradual transitions. Make one false ending, restart with the shortest module, and finish on a simultaneous dry cut with no feedback tail.
```
