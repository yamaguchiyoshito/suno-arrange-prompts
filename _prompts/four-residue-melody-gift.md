```text
Create instrumental neo-soul at 88 BPM from one original sixteen-note melody on steady eighth notes. Distribute its exact pitches and registers among four instruments by note number: electric piano receives 1,5,9,13; clean guitar receives 2,6,10,14; bass guitar receives 3,7,11,15; FM bell receives 4,8,12,16. Each instrument must remain silent at the other twelve positions and may not add pickup, sustain across a foreign slot, double, ornament, or octave-shift its assigned notes.

First present each residue voice alone for two bars in piano-guitar-bass-bell order, preserving all gaps. Then combine all four for eight bars so the complete melody becomes audible for the first time over light drums. End by removing the voices in reverse entry order without replaying a solo. Use wholly original melody, harmony, performance, recordings, and sounds. Do not imitate Aru-2, the referenced track, its exact groove, timbres, riff, recording, or samples.
```
