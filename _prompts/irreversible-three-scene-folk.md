```text
Create an irreversible three-scene progressive-folk suite with original words and melody. Scene one uses free-time lead vocal and fingerpicked acoustic guitar; breaths set phrase length and percussion stays silent. Hard-splice into scene two: a six-beat cycle of strummed guitar and live drums under shorter, urgent vocal lines. At the next boundary, remove that rhythm section forever and let a small horn group trade loose calls around fragments of the vocal contour. Carry one new three-note interval shape through all scenes, changing register and instrument so it becomes a hidden seam, not a hook. Once a scene ends, its meter, texture, or phrasing may not return. Avoid a chorus, symmetric verses, crossfades, risers, borrowed folk melodies, and quoted lyrics. End inside an unanswered horn phrase without restoring guitar or reaching a tonic.
```
