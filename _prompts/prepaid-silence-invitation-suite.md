```text
Create a 32-bar instrumental experimental hip-hop suite at 78 BPM with four parts. Drums must remain silent for bars 1-2, then play their first uninterrupted phrase for exactly two bars. Synth bass waits four full bars, then states its first phrase for exactly four bars. Electric piano waits eight bars, then states its first phrase for exactly eight bars. Analog pad waits sixteen bars, then states its first phrase for exactly sixteen bars. Thus their first entrances are bars 3, 5, 9, and 17.

After completing its required first phrase, each earlier part may continue with related original material through bar 32, but may never leave and re-enter. No preview, pickup, reverb tail, or sidechain trace of a part may occur during its prepaid silence. Build toward the final four-part overlap without a fade. Use only original composition, performances, recordings, and synthesis. Do not imitate Sweet William, the referenced track, its groove, timbres, riffs, recording, or samples.
```
