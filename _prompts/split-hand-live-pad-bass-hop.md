Create a 91 BPM instrumental hip-hop performance recorded in one continuous take. The left hand may play only four fixed pads—kick, snare, closed hat, and one original chord chop—while the right hand may play only a monophonic bass keyboard. Neither hand may cross into the other's role. Use no sequencing, overdubs, quantization, sustain pedal, or prerecorded automation. When the left hand reaches the distant chord pad, the right hand must hold its current bass note rather than add a new attack. Build 32 bars through touch and omission alone. Use wholly original sounds and phrases; do not reuse or imitate any existing recording.
