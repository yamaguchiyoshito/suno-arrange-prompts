```text
Jangly indie-pop arrangement with a warm, melancholic lift.
Keep the original bittersweet mood, but make it slightly more spacious and cinematic.

Use clean electric guitars with chorus, bright arpeggios, and soft 12-string-style layers.
Bass should be melodic, walking around the chords rather than simply following roots.
Drums are steady and dry: tight kick, snare, light tambourine, no heavy fills.

Add subtle string pads in the chorus, not dramatic, just glowing underneath.
Vocal should be intimate, slightly detached, with a close mic feel and short plate reverb.
Add gentle backing harmonies only on phrase endings.

Form: nostalgic intro → restrained verse → open, glowing chorus → quiet outro.
Keep it romantic, fatalistic, and strangely comforting.
```
