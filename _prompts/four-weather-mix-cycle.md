```text
Compose an original eight-bar psychedelic-pop performance at 86 BPM for lead vocal, bass, drums, guitar, and organ, then repeat that exact performance through four mix-weather states. Do not add, remove, or rewrite notes between states.

Bars 1–8 are close mono with almost no room. Bars 9–16 are wide stereo but completely dry. Bars 17–24 use narrow stereo and bright 300-millisecond early reflections with no tail. Bars 25–32 sound distant through dark six-second dub reverb and filtered delay. Repeat all four states once in the same order, keeping source timing, source dynamics, master loudness, and arrangement identical. Only panorama, reflection pattern, filtering, and reverb time may change. Finish by snapping from the distant state to one bar of close mono. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
