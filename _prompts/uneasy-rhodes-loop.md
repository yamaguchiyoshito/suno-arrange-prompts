```text
Minimal electronic arrangement: slow, hypnotic, and emotionally detached, built around a warm but uneasy electric piano loop.

Use a repeating Rhodes or Wurlitzer chord figure with slightly detuned pitch, soft tremolo, and subtle tape wobble. Keep the harmony static and ambiguous, avoiding clear resolution.

Add a low, rounded synth bass that enters sparingly, more like pressure than groove. Keep bass notes stable and restrained: no wobble, no rubbery bends, no exaggerated pitch modulation, and no busy fills. Let it sit as controlled sub pressure.

Drums are minimal: muted electronic kick, dry rim clicks, reversed noise swells, and tiny glitch fragments. Avoid a full beat; let silence and repetition create tension.

Vocals are intimate but processed: close dry lead, chopped fragments, pitch-shifted doubles, and reversed syllables.

Structure: loop → fractured vocal → wider lift → stripped breakdown → unresolved fade.
```
