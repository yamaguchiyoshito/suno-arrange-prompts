```text
Build deep-pocket neo-soul where timing creates the hook.
Drums: dry kick, muted snare, ghost notes, and closed hats. Let the snare sit late while selected hats lean forward; never hard-quantize the kit.
Bass: warm electric bass in short syncopated phrases. Place most attacks behind the kick, but push occasional pickups ahead.
Guitar: muted sixteenth-note chord fragments, ahead of the snare and quieter than the bass. Insert rests instead of constant strumming.
Use sparse electric-piano stabs and organ shadows.
Vocal: intimate, conversational, and low in the pocket; double only key phrases.
Maintain one compact groove. Create movement by muting a part for two beats, not by adding layers.
Mix dry and close: centered bass and drums, narrow guitar, little reverb. Preserve the timing disagreements; they are the groove.
```
