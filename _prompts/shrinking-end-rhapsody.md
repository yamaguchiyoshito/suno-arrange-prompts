Create an instrumental hip-hop rhapsody using Nujabes' “World's End Rhapsody” only as a high-level structural reference. Compose one original twelve-bar loop for piano, drums, bass, and newly recorded textures. Play it once in full. On the second pass replace bars 10–12 with absolute silence; on the third replace bars 7–12; on the fourth replace bars 4–12. Keep each pass exactly twelve bars long, preserve bars that remain down to every note and velocity, and allow no reverb or delay tail to cross into a silenced bar. Never restore a removed bar or add a transition. During the final pass only bars 1–3 sound, followed by nine silent bars. After that silence, replay only the first downbeat as a dry piano chord and cut it after one eighth note. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate Nujabes or the source track.
