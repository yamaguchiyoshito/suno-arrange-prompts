```text
Create industrial electronic pop at 112 BPM from four new object sounds: pipe, bottle, chain, and wood block. In each verse, use them dry and short as kick, backbeat, subdivision, and syncopated accent beneath one original vocal. In each chorus, remove those hits and stretch, tune, and sustain the same four families into bass, chord tones, upper drone, and counterline. Add no synth pad; the vocal pulse keeps time. At transitions, let one object assume its new role one beat early, then move the other three together. Alternate verse and chorus twice, exchanging object roles on the second cycle. Keep four sources and constant master level. Avoid added drums, risers, fills, key changes, and a final crash. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
