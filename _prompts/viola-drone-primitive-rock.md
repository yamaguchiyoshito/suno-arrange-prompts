```text
Create primitive drone rock around detuned electric guitar, abrasive bowed viola, floor tom, bass, and restrained vocal. Hold one viola drone through most of the track, allowing pitch drift, bow noise, and rough harmonics. Repeat a two-chord guitar figure with identical downstrokes; bass doubles only roots. Use a blunt two-beat floor-tom pattern with no cymbals, fills, or swing. Keep the vocal narrow and almost spoken, never doubled. Begin with guitar and drone, add voice after eight bars, then bass and tom without changing harmony. In each section intensify one element: bow pressure, guitar distortion, or tom weight. Never raise all three together. Remove drums near the end while viola and guitar reach maximum friction. Finish as the drone collapses below pitch. Avoid polished reverb, keyboards, backing vocals, solos, and chorus lift.
```
