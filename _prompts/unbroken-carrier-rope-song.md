```text
Create a sparse vocal hip-hop piece using EVISBEATS' “Ippon no Rope” only as a high-level structural reference. Write one continuous monophonic melody and pass it in order through muted acoustic guitar, flute, a single human voice, and electric piano. Their ownership lengths must be 8, 5, 3, and 2 bars, then 2, 3, 5, and 8 bars on the return. At every handoff, the outgoing note must end at the exact instant the incoming note begins; no crossfade, overlap, rest, octave doubling, or accompaniment may obscure the continuous line. The voice may sing only newly written words on its assigned segment. Underneath, use a low kick on every fourth beat and no snare, chords, or counterline. End when the returning guitar completes its eight bars. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate EVISBEATS or the source track.
```
