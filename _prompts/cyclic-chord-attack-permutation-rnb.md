Create a cool 92 BPM alternative R&B track around one original four-note analog-pad chord. Never strike the chord simultaneously. Place its four attacks at fixed sixteenth-note positions 1, 4, 9, and 14 in every bar. In bar one, assign the chord tones from lowest to highest across those positions. At each new bar, rotate only the pitch assignments one position left; bar five must restore bar one's ordering. Preserve attack positions, durations, timbre, velocity, chord tones, drums, sub-bass, and tempo. Add an original restrained vocal spanning the four-bar cycle without marking rotations. Repeat exactly four cycles, then end on position 14 before the next restored ordering. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
