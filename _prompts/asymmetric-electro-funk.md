```text
Create instrumental electro-funk from clipped synth bass, dry drums, metallic percussion, and a short monophonic sequence. Keep rigid 4/4, but create asymmetry with missing sixteenth notes. Write a two-bar bass cell and preserve pitch order, register, and accents. Every four bars, move exactly one rest to another subdivision; never add notes or rewrite the phrase. Apply the rule separately to a one-bar metal pattern, alternating hits across stereo. Keep the kick sparse and fixed. Start with bass, add metal hits after eight bars, then a thin upper sequence. In the middle break, remove kick and sequence while both rest-shifting patterns continue. Return all layers for one final cycle without thickening. End by deleting bass attacks one by one until a single metal hit remains. Avoid pads, fills, solos, chord washes, and risers.
```
