```text
Create a process-minimal vocal work that simulates recursive room coloration across seven consecutive sections. Begin with one original spoken paragraph, close and dry. Repeat its duration, pauses, average level, and voice position exactly in every section. On each return, reduce consonant clarity by one fixed step, lengthen vowel decay, strengthen the same three room-resonance bands, and add slightly more playback noise. Render the audible result of this process, not an actual recording procedure. Use hard cuts between sections and make no changes within a section. Let recognizable words become vowels, sustained tones, then near-pure resonance. End after one resonance-only section and several seconds of room tone. Avoid accompaniment, extra voices, pitch correction, percussion, crossfades, and dramatic mastering.
```
