```text
Build a shadowy trip-hop arrangement around a dark electric-piano progression with wide gaps between chords.
Use an extremely slow, heavy beat: a soft deep kick, a dry rim-like snare, and only occasional closed hi-hat pulses.
Hold a low sub-bass note beneath each harmonic section, letting its weight decay naturally instead of writing an active bass line.
Keep the lead vocal close, fragile, and nearly dry; after every important phrase, leave a long silence before any instrument answers.
Do not fill those gaps with decorative percussion, backing vocals, or chord extensions.
Introduce low strings only in the final third, entering late behind the beat with long notes and restrained dissonance.
Avoid a conventional lift in the chorus; increase tension through bass weight, longer reverberation tails, and denser string voicings.
End with the electric piano and sustained low tone unresolved, allowing the last vocal silence to remain exposed.
```
