```text
Create hushed dream pop at 78 BPM with one lead singer, slow bass, brushed drums, and floating clean guitar. Write verses in eight lines of equal two-bar length and alternate two mutually exclusive vocal distances.

Odd-numbered lines are close, dry, centered, and full-bandwidth, with no doubled voice. Even-numbered lines are recorded as a separate distant tone, low-pass filtered above 3 kHz, placed far back, and followed by one quiet quarter-note delay. Never overlap the two voices: all dry sound and effects from one line must end before the next line starts. Keep the band pattern unchanged so only vocal depth alternates. In the last verse, reverse which distance receives odd and even lines, then end with two full bars after the distant tail disappears. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
