Create an instrumental broken hip-hop groove using mabanua's “Tangled Up” only as a high-level structural reference. On a sixteen-slot grid, place pattern A on slots 1, 4, 7, 10, and 13, and pattern B on slots 2, 5, 8, 11, and 14. Give A a muted-guitar timbre and B a short synth-bass timbre. Treat the ten time-ordered attacks as five adjacent A-B pairs. Every four bars swap the timbre ownership of exactly one pair, moving from the leftmost pair to the rightmost. Never move an onset, alter a pitch, change a velocity, add an attack, or swap more than one pair per section. After five swaps, all pairs read B-A; then undo one pair per section from right to left. Keep a sparse kick-snare frame unchanged and stop when the original A-B braid returns. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate mabanua or the source track.
