```text
Arrange an original live indie-rock performance for exactly six players: voice/keys, guitar/sampler, bass/saxophone, keys/guitar, left-side drums, and right-side drums. Use no synchronized backing track. A player may perform only one listed role at a time.

Begin with one role per player and keep the ensemble count fixed. Change sections through audible handoffs: a keyboard sustains one note while its player stops and another player continues that function; a drummer transfers the pulse to guitar muting before changing instrument; bass passes its register to saxophone. Never add a seventh layer or let a player overlap roles. Reserve full six-person unison for one final attack, then leave a single human voice. Use wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy an existing melody, lyric, live arrangement signature, performance, recording, or sample.
```
