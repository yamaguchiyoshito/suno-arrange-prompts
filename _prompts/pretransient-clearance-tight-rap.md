```text
Create a 93 BPM original vocal hip-hop track with hard drums, short electric-piano chords, and mono bass. Every non-drum event must end at least 20 ms before the next kick, snare, or hat attack, leaving a measurable silent clearance window around each drum transient. Reverb, delay, legato overlap, sidechain tails, and cymbal wash are forbidden. Keep the drum pattern fixed and let the arrangement breathe by shortening musical notes, never by moving drum hits. Deliver a dry original rap without doubles. Compose all lyrics, rhythms, harmony, and sounds from scratch; avoid recognizable flows, samples, or vocal likenesses.
```
