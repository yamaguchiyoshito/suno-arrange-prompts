```text
Create analog psychedelic pop governed by two mix states. Write a short original verse melody over a repeating synth-bass figure and two slow pad chords. Preserve the notes, note count, tempo, and performance intensity in both states.

State A is narrow mono, dry, midrange-only, and slightly overdriven. State B is wide stereo, full-range, and followed by a dark tail. Alternate A and B every sixteen bars, then eight, four, two, and one, changing only bandwidth, space, and placement. Keep the vocal dry and close in A; spread only its unchanged ambience outward in B. All words must be new. Add no fill, key change, countermelody, or drop. At maximum switching speed, freeze in A and let the bass end an incomplete loop. Never borrow an existing melody, lyric, signature chord movement, riff, recording, vocal likeness, or sample.
```
