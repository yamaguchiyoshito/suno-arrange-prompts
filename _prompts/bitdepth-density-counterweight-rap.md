Create vocal digital abstract hip-hop at 92 BPM with rigid dry drums, centered sub bass, aggressive rap, and one bank of original electronic fragments. First normalize all sixteen fragments to equal integrated energy. Keep drums, sub, tempo, harmony, and master level unchanged. Transform only the fragment bank through four four-bar stages.

Use 16-bit fragments with sixteen events at base gain, then 12-bit with eight events at +3 dB, 8-bit with four at +6 dB, and 6-bit with two at +9 dB. At each reduction, delete every even-numbered surviving event. Keep each survivor at its original attack position and original duration; use the gain steps only to conserve the fragment bank's integrated energy. Leave enough headroom to prevent clipping. Do not replace deleted attacks or add distortion layers. After the two-event stage, stop immediately without restoring resolution. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
