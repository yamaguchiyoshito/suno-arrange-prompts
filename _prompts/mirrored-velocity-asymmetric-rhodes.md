```text
Create instrumental neo-soul beat music at 84 BPM around a twelve-note electric-piano loop. Assign its MIDI velocities exactly as 30,45,60,80,100,115,115,100,80,60,45,30, making the dynamics perfectly mirrored. Starting on step 0 of a sixteen-step bar, separate consecutive notes by this circular list of sixteenth-step intervals: 1,1,2,1,1,2,1,3,1,1,1,1. The intervals sum to sixteen but are deliberately not mirrored.

Repeat the one-bar loop without quantization changes. Add restrained drum-machine hits and a warm original synth-bass phrase, but keep them out of the Rhodes register. In a middle eight bars, remove drums so the contrast between symmetric velocity and asymmetric timing is exposed; change no Rhodes note, interval, or velocity. Use wholly original pitches, harmony, performance, recording, and sound design. Do not imitate Aru-2, Kzyboost, the referenced track, its exact groove, timbres, riffs, recording, or samples.
```
