```text
Create a 96 BPM nocturnal electropop track lasting exactly 35 numbered bars. Keep one quiet drum-machine pattern and one sustained sub-bass note active throughout. Assign four fixed one-bar loops to prime numbers: muted guitar=2, glassy FM synth=3, music-box bell=5, and wordless vocal chord=7. A loop may sound in bar n only when its prime divides n exactly; otherwise mute that loop and its reverb completely. When several primes divide the same bar, layer their unchanged loops. Never transpose, vary, fill, fade, anticipate, or replace any loop. Let bar 1 contain only the foundation, and end at bar 35 after the bell and vocal loops finish. Use wholly original harmony, melody, voices, recordings, and sounds; do not imitate any identifiable song, hook, arrangement, or vocal likeness.
```
