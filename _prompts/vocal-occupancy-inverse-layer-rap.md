```text
Create a 78 BPM vocal abstract hip-hop track in 4/4. Divide every bar into sixteen sixteenth-note slots and write wholly original rap first. Mark every slot crossed by an audible vocal onset or sustain as occupied; let V be that count. Place exactly 16−V accompaniment attacks in the remaining slots, one attack per slot, never under voice. Assign those attacks left to right by a continuous drums→bass guitar→single electric-key cycle that does not reset at barlines. Keep each carrier's sound, pitch set, velocity, and duration fixed. In the final three bars, predetermine V as 4, then 8, then 12 to expose the complement rule, and stop after the last occupied slot. Use entirely original melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable flows, riffs, or vocal likenesses.
```
