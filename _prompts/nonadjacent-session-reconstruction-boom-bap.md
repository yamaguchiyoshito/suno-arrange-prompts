```text
Create a 92 BPM vocal boom-bap track from four original eight-bar live-band takes A-D, each containing drums, bass, and electric piano. Split every take into two-bar positions 1-4, then assemble all sixteen pieces exactly once as A1-B2-C3-D4 / B1-C2-D3-A4 / C1-D2-A3-B4 / D1-A2-B3-C4. Adjacent pieces may share neither a take letter nor a source position. Use hard edits with no crossfades and place one continuous original rap performance over the reconstruction. Do not add fills to hide boundaries. Compose and record all material yourself; avoid existing samples, melodies, lyrics, flows, or vocal likenesses.
```
