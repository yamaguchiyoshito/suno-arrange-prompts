```text
Record an original 78 BPM performance of drums, bass, electric guitar, and piano, then mute every direct track for the entire finished piece. Use those hidden tracks only as control signals: the kick envelope ducks continuous low noise, the snare opens a 2 kHz noise gate for 80 milliseconds, guitar pitch drives a resonator bank, and piano velocity sets the decay of four synthetic chimes. The audible mix may contain only the controlled noise, resonances, and chimes; no dry or wet return from the source instruments is allowed. Introduce the four control relationships one at a time, combine them for sixteen bars, then remove them in reverse order. Keep the hidden performance unedited. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
