Create an 84 BPM vocal psychedelic abstract hip-hop track from one original seven-note scale. Initially, synth bass owns degrees 1 and 5; electric keys own 2, 4, and 6; horn or tuned-metal hits own 3 and 7. Include at least one instrumental occurrence of every degree. The original voice must use the same scale. In each eight-bar block, count vocal note attacks by degree, excluding degrees already transferred; ties go to the lower degree. Transfer only the winner to the next owner in the cycle bass→keys→horn/metal→bass. Move every occurrence of that degree to the new timbre while preserving position, register, duration, and velocity, retaining at least one audible occurrence. Transfer all seven degrees once, then end with an unpitched hit. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
