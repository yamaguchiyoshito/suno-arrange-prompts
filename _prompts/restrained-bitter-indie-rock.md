```text
Verse is restrained: clean electric + light acoustic, simple triads with add9; keep space. Vocal is dry, intimate, centered; minimal compression.

Chorus lifts via density, not volume: add drums (steady kick, soft snare), bass (root + passing tones), and a second guitar with light drive. Keep rhythms straight; no swing.

Guitars: one clean, one slightly overdriven; use subtle chorus/reverb for width, avoid wash. Add faint synth pad for glue.

Harmony stays diatonic with occasional IV–iv color; avoid overextended chords.

Form: clear verse/chorus contrast. Build by layering, not big dynamic jumps.

Mix: tight low end, clean 200–400 Hz. Vocals forward; guitars spread L/R. Short plate + small room; keep clarity over ambience. Emotion: candid, controlled, slightly bitter.
```
