Create an original instrumental ambient-electronic piece at 88 BPM. Use rounded synth bass, soft brushed percussion, a quiet slowly changing pad, and a warm FM-synth motif of three notes, played once every four bars. Keep the groove light and gently syncopated, with open space between percussion hits. For the first sixteen bars, let the synth play all three notes. For the next sixteen bars, play only its first two notes and give the third to a soft vibraphone at exactly the same pitch and time; never double that note. Keep the motif rhythm, bass line, and percussion unchanged so the different decay becomes the focus. In an eight-bar coda, let the synth reclaim all three notes while the percussion gradually fades. Leave the last synth tone and pad to decay naturally. Warm coastal light, gentle motion, clear low end, and quiet detail without a large crescendo. Compose and record original melodies, harmony, and sounds.
