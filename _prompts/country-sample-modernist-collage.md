```text
Create modernist electronic Americana from an original vocal narrative and one newly recorded pedal-steel fragment. Repeat it unchanged; treat it as a displaced sample, not accompaniment. Place a dry live-tom strike and compact synth-bass note around its tail, never at the same instant. Begin with steel answering the voice, tom marking sentence endings, and bass filling empty downbeats. Every eight bars, exchange those three functions while preserving the same sparse event count. Keep the lead vocal low, close, and rhythmically independent, with newly written words. Use abrupt edits, brief reverse tails, and narrow mono placement. Avoid lush strings, acoustic strumming, trap hats, blues clichés, solos, chorus form, sampled songs, and sung quotation. End with the steel fragment alone, withholding its expected drum and bass replies.
```
