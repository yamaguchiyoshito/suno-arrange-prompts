```text
Create alternative electronic R&B at 104 BPM around one original violin pitch cell and restrained vocal melody. In each A section, use the cell only as pizzicato percussion: low plucks on downbeats, muted taps on backbeats, harmonics on subdivisions. Add no drums. In each B, stop all pizzicato and bow the same pitches into sustained harmony with no new notes. A dry machine beat inherits every former onset and accent: kick for low plucks, rim for taps, hat for harmonics. Alternate equal eight-bar A and B blocks while vocal rhythm and melody stay fixed. Allow bow and pizzicato together only on the final beat, then stop. Avoid a separate bass line, string flourishes, fills, stacked vocals, and modulation. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
