```text
Create an original instrumental ambient-jazz piece at 64 BPM. Use warm Rhodes close to the listener, a soft muted trumpet in the distance, low sustained bass, and a barely audible brushed pulse. Shape each eight-bar passage as a quiet conversation: Rhodes presents a short phrase in bars one to four, the trumpet answers with two or three sustained notes in bars five and six, and neither plays new notes in bars seven and eight. Let the trumpet's long, dark reverb remain audible during that space while the bass and brushed pulse continue softly. Begin with eight bars of Rhodes and pulse, then four of these conversational passages. End by removing the pulse and bass after the final answer, leaving only its reverb tail. Keep the Rhodes relatively dry and the trumpet soft-edged. Use restrained extended harmony, natural breath, and even dynamics. All melodies, phrasing, and recordings should be newly created.
```
