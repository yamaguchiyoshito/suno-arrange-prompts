```text
Compose a beatless piece using one dry lead voice and exactly three processed harmony voices; use no instruments, drums, or environmental sounds. Write new lyrics whose syllables clearly alternate open vowels, closed vowels, and consonant endings.

Switch harmony by pronunciation only. On open vowels, place the processed voices at fifth, octave, and upper fifth. On closed vowels, compress them into neighboring seconds around the lead. At consonant-ended syllables, collapse all four voices into unison before the release. Change voicing only at syllable boundaries, never midway through a vowel. Let one late phrase move rapidly through all three states, then finish with the dry lead alone. Create wholly original words, melody, and harmonization; do not imitate an existing lyric, melodic contour, signature vocal processing, performance, recording, or sample.
```
