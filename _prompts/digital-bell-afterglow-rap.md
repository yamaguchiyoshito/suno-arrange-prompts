```text
Create an original Japanese melodic rap track at 90 BPM. Pair short digital FM bells with a deep rounded synth bass, dry snare, sparse kicks, and lightly swung hats. Keep the lead close and understated, moving between low conversational rap and gently sustained sung endings. Use subtle pitch correction as a smooth electronic color while keeping the words clear. In each sixteen-bar verse, let an original three-note bell motif answer only in the gaps between vocal phrases. In each eight-bar hook, repeat that same three-note cell every bar under a simple sung refrain. Hold the bass low and the upper register spacious. Before the last hook, remove the drums for four bars and leave voice, bells, and bass. Return with quiet vocal doubles and finish on a lone bell decay. Wistful, private, late-night atmosphere; clean low end and soft digital shimmer. Use newly composed melodies, original vocal phrasing, and fresh recordings.
```
