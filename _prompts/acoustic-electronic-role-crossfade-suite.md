```text
Create instrumental jazz-fusion at 112 BPM with acoustic and electronic versions of four roles: live versus programmed drums, electric versus synth bass, horns versus FM voices, and piano versus sequenced keys. Open with live drums and horns plus synth bass and sequenced keys. Keep exactly one version of each role audible. Every sixteen bars, crossfade two roles in opposite directions: one acoustic becomes electronic while one electronic becomes acoustic. Preserve notes, rhythm, register, and energy; change only material and articulation. Never switch all roles together or exceed four layers. Visit four states, then hold the inverse of the opening for eight bars. Avoid solos, fills, pads, tempo changes, and a final unison. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
