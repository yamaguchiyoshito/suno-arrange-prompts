```text
Create an experimental duet for a natural lead voice and breathy tenor saxophone over a nearly inaudible vocal drone. Give each soloist a different original two-bar cell: the voice uses one rhythm and pitch contour, the sax another. Present them separately, never as simultaneous leads.

On the next cycle, exchange only their structural properties. The voice inherits the saxophone's sequence of rises, falls, and repeated pitches while keeping its own words; the saxophone inherits the voice's exact attack-and-rest rhythm while choosing new pitches. Alternate foreground ownership every two bars. In the final cycle, exchange back but remove the last event from each cell, creating paired absences. End before either completes its phrase. Use wholly original music and lyrics; never copy an existing melody, lyric, saxophone phrase, vocal signature, recording, or sample.
```
