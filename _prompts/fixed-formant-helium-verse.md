Create a slow vocal hip-hop piece using MASS-HOLE's “helium” only as a high-level point of departure. Write and record one original one-line rap refrain. End on the final consonant with no reverb tail. Use only newly created material: every sound, melody, lyric, performance, and recording must be original; do not imitate MASS-HOLE or the source track, and do not reuse its riffs, samples, or vocal phrasing.
