```text
Create an instrumental call-and-response dub beat using 16FLIP's “Whistle Dub” only as a high-level point of departure. Perform a newly composed four-pitch whistle motif once every two bars. Each whistle onset must trigger a notch in the sustained synth bass, centered on that whistle's fundamental frequency, exactly 12 dB deep at Q=8 for one quarter note. Close the notch instantly after one beat. The bass pitch and volume must remain fixed, so the whistle is answered by a temporary spectral hole rather than another note. Cycle the four whistle pitches in order 1, 5, flat 3, 2 relative to a new tonic, repeat four times, and do not add whistle echoes. Drums maintain one unchanged sparse pattern. In the coda, omit the whistles but execute the four memorized notches in rhythm, leaving audible negative-space answers to absent calls. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate 16FLIP or the source track.
```
