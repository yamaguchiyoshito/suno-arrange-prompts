```text
Sparse indie-emo / dream-pop ballad built around felt piano, clean electric guitar, and intimate vocals.
Start with a fragile single-note piano motif, using maj7, add9, and sus2 chords that never fully resolve.
Keep the verse nearly weightless: close dry vocal, soft room noise, reversed guitar swells, no drums.
In the pre-chorus, spread delayed arpeggiated guitars wide while the bass holds long root notes.

The chorus should bloom, not explode: brushed snare, soft kick, cymbal swells, and one faint high harmony above the lead vocal.
Add subtle suitcase-like textures: muted thuds, metal clicks, floor scrapes, distant station ambience.
In the final section, remove layers instead of building bigger, returning to piano, voice, and long reverb.
End unresolved, with the room ringing after the voice disappears.
```
