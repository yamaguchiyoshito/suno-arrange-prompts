```text
Four-on-the-floor kick as the core; 16th-note hats, light laid-back snare. Drums are mostly programmed with a thin acoustic layer.

Bass is synth-led, sustained, lightly sidechained for a subtle pump. Gtr1: clean with dotted-8th delay, looping a short motif. Gtr2: low-cut distorted wall for width, minimal chords.

Synths: analog pads filling mids; slow filter sweeps. Harmony: major base with add9/sus; avoid hard cadences.

Twin vocals: one leads; the other doubles very low or adds an octave below; widen only in chorus.

Build with 8-bar loops. Mix: tight lows, clean 200–400 Hz; vocals centered/close, others wide; long reverb with pre-delay. Cool, nostalgic, slightly mechanical.
```
