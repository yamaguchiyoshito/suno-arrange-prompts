```text
Create vocal glitch-pop from electric piano, elastic synth bass, micro-edited drums, and an original topline. Define six related keyboard voicings, then switch to a different one every one or two beats while one bass tone anchors each bar. Cut the drum pattern into short kick, snare, and hat cells; restart one cell before the bar line, but keep a clear quarter-note reference underneath. Present the first vocal phrase intact. Later, reuse only newly recorded breaths, consonants, and vowel tails as pitched replies across stereo, never as a quoted hook. Alternate eight dense bars with two thin bars of voice, bass, and one key attack. Do not add risers, guitar walls, long pads, or a conventional solo. In the final passage, remove edits one layer at a time until the uncut voice and electric piano remain, then stop on an unresolved voicing.
```
