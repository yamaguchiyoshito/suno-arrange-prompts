```text
Create a warm, laid-back hip-hop song using EVISBEATS' “Ii Jikan” only as a high-level point of departure. Build four consecutive eight-bar sections around drums, synth bass, electric piano, and muted acoustic guitar. Across every section, those instruments must produce exactly 32 attacks in total. Start with attack allocations of 12, 8, 8, and 4. At each boundary transfer exactly two attacks clockwise: drums to bass, bass to piano, piano to guitar, then guitar to drums. Keep harmony, tempo, and average loudness fixed so the changing ownership of density is the audible form. Place a newly written two-line rap only in bars 5–8 of each section, with no ad-libs elsewhere. End immediately when the original 12/8/8/4 allocation returns. Use only newly created material: every sound, melody, lyric, performance, and recording must be original; do not imitate EVISBEATS or the source track, and do not reuse its riffs, samples, or vocal phrasing.
```
