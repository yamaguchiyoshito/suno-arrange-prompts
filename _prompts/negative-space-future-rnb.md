```text
Create minimal future R&B defined by negative space and asymmetrical percussion. Place an intimate, near-whispered vocal over a short sub-bass phrase. Build the beat from muted kick, dry snare, rim clicks, hand taps, and one original mouth-made sound; use no recognizable samples. Keep 4/4, but leave beat one empty in alternating bars and answer vocal phrases with isolated sixteenth-note hits. Start with voice, bass, and two drum sounds. Every eight bars, replace one percussion color rather than stacking layers. Keep the chorus equally quiet; mark it by doubling selected final words and moving one bass rest an eighth-note later. Drop to voice and rim click for four bars, then restore the initial pattern. End with an exposed vocal fragment and one sub-bass note. Avoid pads, strings, cymbal washes, dense chords, ad-libs, and risers.
```
