```text
Create precise electronic pop on a sixteen-step grid where each subdivision has one exclusive owner. Assign FM synth to steps 1, 5, 9, and 13; synth bass to 3, 7, 11, and 15; divide the rest among kick, snare, and digital ticks. Never allow two instrumental attacks on one step. Add a clear lead vocal with a wholly original melody, using held notes and pickups across the grid. Every eight bars, rotate ownership while preserving the event count: synth takes former bass positions, bass takes percussion positions, and percussion fills vacated synth positions. Change harmony only when step 1 changes owner. Raise energy through brighter tone and wider stereo, not more layers. Avoid strummed instruments, live fills, overlapping arpeggios, swing, key changes, and drops. Complete one last rotation, then stop all parts together on step 1.
```
