```text
Create original vocal indie pop at 102 BPM with dry drums, electric piano, and a five-note bass-guitar cell. Keep the five pitches, note lengths, and order permanently fixed; only its starting point may rotate. After every sung phrase, classify the final vocal movement. If the last sung note rises from the previous note, rotate the next bass statement forward by one position. If it falls, rotate backward by one. If it repeats the pitch, keep the current starting point. Let bass answer during the vocal gap, then hold its new rotation beneath the next phrase. Use eight phrases whose ending directions form an original pattern, followed by an instrumental pass that reveals the resulting rotation history. Do not transpose, reverse, ornament, or reharmonize the bass cell. Avoid fills, key changes, stacked vocals, and independent bass variation. Use newly written lyrics and melody; do not imitate any singer or reuse any existing lyric, melody, signature bass line, recording, or sample.
```
