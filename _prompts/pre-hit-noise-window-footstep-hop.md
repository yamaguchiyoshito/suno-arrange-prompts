```text
Record eight original footstep sounds and place them at fixed irregular positions in a two-bar 86 BPM loop. Hold every footstep at -36 dB beneath a continuous mono noise bed at -24 dB, making the steps inaudible while the noise is present. In four successive eight-bar sections, cut a centered silent window in the noise around every footstep measuring 20, 40, 60, then 80 milliseconds. Do not raise the footsteps or change their timing; only the widening noise windows may reveal them. Keep a sparse kick, one sub-bass note, and an original low-register rap unchanged. After the widest-window section, close every window and end with the hidden loop. Use no sampled footsteps or environmental recordings from existing works. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
