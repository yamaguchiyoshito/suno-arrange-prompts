```text
Create a low-centered alternative R&B arrangement with intimate, conversational vocals. Open with dark electric-piano voicings in the middle-low register, leaving a beat of space between chords. Add rounded sub-bass that sustains roots with rare passing tones. Use a dry half-time beat: muted kick, short rimshot, sparse closed hi-hat, and no cymbals. Keep verses monochrome and let vocal timing follow natural speech. Do not make the chorus louder; widen one piano chord, double only selected final words, and descend the bass while the beat stays fixed. Drop drums for two bars before the last section, then restore only kick and rimshot. Introduce breathy tenor sax solely in the final eight bars, answering vocal gaps without tracing the melody. End unresolved, with the sub-bass fading before the piano.
```
