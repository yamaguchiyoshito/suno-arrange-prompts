```text
BPM 120–130, near four-on-the-floor with slight human swing. Drums: steady kick, 16th hats, light backbeat; layer acoustic + programmed. Bass: synth-led, sustained, gently sidechained to the kick for pulse.

Gtr1: clean with chorus/delay, repeating a simple motif. Gtr2: low-cut distorted “wall” for width. Keys: analog pads + arpeggiator to fill upper mids. Harmony: major base with add9/sus; avoid hard cadences to keep the loop.

Twin vocals: one leads; the other doubles softly or adds an octave below; open wider only in the chorus.

Mix: tighten lows, clean 200–400 Hz. Guitars/synths wide; vocals centered and close. Longer reverb with pre-delay for depth. Tone: cool, nostalgic, slightly mechanical.
```
