```text
Keep it sparse. Center on Rhodes piano + clean guitar. Piano plays broken chords with light high-note ornaments; leave space.
Guitar uses dotted-8th delay and light reverb, repeating a simple motif, panned wide.

Vocal is very close and intimate; light compression, short plate, mostly dry.
Add a very subtle double only in the chorus; harmonies (3rds) used sparingly.

Bass is minimal, root-focused, low in the mix. Drums enter late with brushes/light sticks; soft kick, dry snare.

Form: “quiet → slight swell → quiet.” Harmony: maj7/add9 colors, avoid firm cadences.
Mix: clean 200–2kHz, restrained low end; gentle hall + plate for depth without over-widening.
```
