```text
Tense indie-rock arrangement with nervous forward motion, built around interlocking guitars and a dry, urgent groove.

Add clean electric guitar arpeggios that weave around the rhythm instead of doubling it. Keep the harmony restless, using suspended chords and quick passing tones to create a feeling of being pulled forward.

Bass should be melodic but tight, following the kick and pushing into transitions. Drums stay dry and propulsive: tight kick, snare rim/ghost notes, closed hi-hats, and light ride only near the final lift.

Form: quiet pulse → layered guitars → rising vocal tension → full-band release → abrupt, unresolved ending. Keep the mix lean, fast, and claustrophobic.
```
