```text
Create live-band vocal abstract hip-hop at 90 BPM with rap, bass guitar, dry drums, Wurlitzer, and muted electric guitar. Compose one original six-note, two-bar phrase containing varied rhythm, dynamics, and rests. Never perform it intact after the opening.

Decompose its attributes: bass plays the pitch order at equal intervals; drums reproduce only the original rhythm on one neutral sound; Wurlitzer renders the loud-soft sequence on one repeated pitch; guitar sustains one note for the phrase's total rest duration. Repeat that composite for four cycles. Then expose bass, drums, Wurlitzer, and guitar alone for one two-bar cycle each, in that order, while the other three remain silent. End when the guitar reaches the exact rest-duration total. Avoid solos, fills, and extra harmony. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
