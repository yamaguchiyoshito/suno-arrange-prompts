```text
Program one original two-bar pattern at 94 BPM with exactly sixteen attacks and sixteen distinct velocities. Rank the attacks from quietest to loudest and divide them into four quartiles Q1-Q4 of four attacks each. In section one map Q1-Q4 to closed hat, rim, snare, and kick. In the next three sections rotate that mapping left once per section, using rim-snare-kick-hat, snare-kick-hat-rim, then kick-hat-rim-snare. Preserve every onset and numerical velocity; only the sample selected by its velocity quartile may change. Keep one bass pattern and the original rap meter identical across all four sixteen-bar sections. Never layer two drum sounds on one attack. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
