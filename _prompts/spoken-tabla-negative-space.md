```text
Create an electroacoustic spoken-word piece from dry voice, tabla, sparse strings, and silence. Use newly written, non-quoted phrases delivered calmly without a sung melody. Let one low tabla stroke anchor the pulse, then answer with compact high-drum cells; never maintain a continuous groove. Place a single cello or violin note only after a spoken phrase, leaving its decay exposed. Cut selected final vowels into short echoes that become rhythmic replies, but keep the words intelligible. Build one middle passage by shortening the gaps among voice, drum, and string, not by increasing volume. Then remove the drum for several phrases so breaths and room tone carry the meter. Avoid chords, bass line, pads, chorus, vocal harmony, and cinematic swells. Finish after an unfinished sentence with one low drum resonance and no cadence.
```
