```text
Create a gentle homeward hip-hop song using EVISBEATS' “Kaerimasho” only as a high-level point of departure. Begin with five layers: lead voice, electric piano, bass guitar, soft drums, and a quiet room-noise texture. At each eight-bar boundary permanently remove one layer in that order. The departing layer must play one final isolated note; at the same onset, pitch, and duration, exactly one remaining pitched instrument takes over that note, making the departure audible without a gap. Do not fade layers, reintroduce them, add replacements, or change tempo. When drums leave, the piano must inherit the final snare rhythm as one muted chord. When room noise leaves, preserve silence rather than replacing it. Finish with eight bars of piano alone and stop before its last decay resolves. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate EVISBEATS or the source track.
```
