Create instrumental beat music at 108 BPM from one newly recorded dry palm tap. Every audible event must be a copy of that single file; add no oscillator, noise source, second recording, or stock sample. Derive four roles only by editing copies: kick at -24 semitones with a 120 ms decay; snare at original pitch, high-passed at 1.5 kHz with an 80 ms decay; hat at +24 semitones with a 25 ms decay; bass from a 40 ms tail segment looped with crossfades and repitched to an original three-note line.

Build a precise 16-step broken beat and let the four derivatives overlap, proving that one impact supplies rhythm and low melody. In the bridge, solo each role for one bar in kick-snare-hat-bass order, then restore the full pattern. Use only original composition, performance, recording, and processing. Do not imitate doooo, the referenced track, its exact groove, timbres, riffs, recording, or samples.
