```text
Write an original progressive-folk song for close vocal, acoustic guitar, restrained tom, and low brass. Keep the sung section concise and harmonically plain. On the final lyric, freeze only its last vowel into a quiet sustained drone; after that instant, no intelligible voice may return.

Grow a 60–90 second wordless coda from that pitch alone. First reveal guitar harmonics related to the vowel, then widely spaced tom strokes, then one low horn tone at a time. Each entry must emerge from the decay of the previous sound. Increase spectral width and harmonic tension without raising tempo or creating a conventional climax. Remove the tom, then the horn, leaving the vowel-colored resonance and one guitar harmonic unresolved. Use entirely new lyrics, melody, and harmony; never imitate an existing song, signature transition, performance, recording, or sample.
```
