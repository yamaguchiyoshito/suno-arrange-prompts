```text
Build hypnotic vocal trance-rock at 100 BPM in steady 4/4 with drums, walking bass, clean guitar, organ, and a calm lead voice. Create one original instrumental phrase exactly six quarter-note beats long and repeat it continuously without resetting at barlines.

Because the six-beat phrase realigns with beat 1 every three 4/4 bars, permit arrangement changes only at those 12-beat landing points. Landing one introduces guitar, landing two replaces it with organ, landing three brings guitar back and removes hi-hat, and landing four restores hi-hat while bass moves up one octave. Between landings, no instrument may enter, leave, change register, or alter its pattern. Continue for 21 bars, then stop everyone together on a landing point that is not an eight-bar boundary. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
