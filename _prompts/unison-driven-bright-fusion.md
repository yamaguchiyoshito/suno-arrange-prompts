```text
Create instrumental jazz fusion with live drums, syncopated bass, electric piano, lead guitar, and a short synth lead. Write a new four-note motif with a sixteenth-note contour. State it first on guitar, then repeat it in exact guitar-synth unison, then split it into two-note call-and-response fragments. Keep the bass independent, alternating fingered pickups and brief slap accents without doubling the motif. Let electric piano use short chord stabs between phrases, never a continuous pad. In the middle, reduce to bass, rimshot, and piano for eight bars; return by passing the motif through each instrument once. Allow one concise guitar variation, but no open-ended solo. Keep articulation clean and dynamics controlled. Finish with the full band playing the original four-note rhythm on newly chosen pitches, followed by one dry stop.
```
