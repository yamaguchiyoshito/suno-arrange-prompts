```text
Arrange intimate bossa nova for nylon-string guitar and close vocal, with no drums.
The thumb supplies the bass pulse. Keep low notes even while upper chords arrive late, creating sway without changing tempo.
Use compact sixth, minor-seventh, add9, and altered-dominant chords. Move inner voices by half-step; avoid bright strumming.
Vocal: near-whispered, dry, and conversational. Let selected consonants touch the thumb pulse while each sustained vowel settles behind the delayed upper chord. No belting, stacked harmony, or ad-libs.
Keep breaths, fret noise, and pauses. Use a short room reflection.
Hold the same low volume and two-part instrumentation throughout. Create contrast through chord color, silence, and microtiming.
No added bass, keys, strings, percussion, dramatic build, or stereo widening. End on a quiet unresolved voicing and preserve its resonance.
```
