```text
Create restrained vocal krautrock around a scaffold that disappears. For eight bars, play a blunt four-hit guide beside motorik drums, one-note bass, muted guitar, and a wholly original two-line vocal. Make every entrance, cutoff, and breath depend on its four positions. Then remove the guide and never restore it. Honor its empty spaces: guitar stops before the missing second hit, bass avoids the third, snare answers the fourth, and the singer breathes where the first would fall. Keep tempo, harmony, and intensity fixed so coordinated gaps reveal the absent pattern. Later, mute one instrument at a time without filling its space, and return each only at its original position. End after every player leaves all four guide positions silent in one bar. Avoid solos, fills, tempo drift, crescendos, borrowed lyrics, and recognizable melodies.
```
