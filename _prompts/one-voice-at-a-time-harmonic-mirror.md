```text
Create a smoky 76 BPM vocal abstract hip-hop track around one original four-voice electric-key chord and fixed axis pitch T. Choose four distinct source pitches, none equal to T, whose mirrors p' = 2T−p remain distinct and playable. Hold the original for eight bars. At each next eight-bar boundary, mirror one additional untouched voice from highest to lowest. This creates four cumulative transformations and five states including the original. Never restore a voice, correct its octave, add doublings, change T, or alter timbre. Keep drums, sub-bass, tempo, key rhythm, dynamics, and sparse original vocal fixed. After the lowest voice is mirrored, sustain the result without drums for two bars and cut. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
