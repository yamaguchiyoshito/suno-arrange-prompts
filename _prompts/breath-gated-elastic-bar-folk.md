```text
Create a bare solo folk performance with one natural lead voice and fingerpicked acoustic guitar. Use no click, percussion, pad, or doubled vocal. Build the accompaniment from a new four-note guitar cell whose internal pulse never accelerates or slows.

Let the singer's breathing alter bar length only: insert one eighth-note rest after an ordinary breath and two after a deep breath, then resume the cell at its exact internal speed. Do not stretch the guitar notes to hide these gaps. Keep harmony nearly static, allowing one bass-note change after every fourth vocal phrase. Make the widest silence follow the most emotionally dense line. End when a final deep breath interrupts the cell before note four. Write wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, fingerpicking signature, vocal mannerism, recording, or sample.
```
