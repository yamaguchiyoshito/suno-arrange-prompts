```text
Create vocal abstract hip-hop at 96 BPM. Record one original four-bar electric-piano performance and cut it chronologically into sixteen equal slices numbered 1 through 16. In each eight-bar verse, choose exactly eight distinct slices and play them only in strictly increasing index order. Skips are allowed, but repetition, descending moves, wraparound, reversal, and time stretching are forbidden. Use a different eight-index subset in every verse.

Keep one firm drum groove and synth-bass line beneath the hard cuts. Hooks may remove the chops and use a newly synthesized chord, but they may not reveal the intact source performance. Finish with all sixteen slices once in natural index order, then stop. Write a completely original rap and sung response. All melody, harmony, lyrics, performances, recordings, and samples must be newly created. Do not imitate DJ PMX, the referenced song, its performers, recognizable flow, riffs, recording, or samples.
```
