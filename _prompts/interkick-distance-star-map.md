```text
Create instrumental synth techno at 116 BPM with an original irregular kick cycle, restrained hats, mono bass, and one dry FM voice. Limit every gap between consecutive kicks to one through seven sixteenth-note steps. Immediately after each kick, play one short FM note whose scale degree equals the length of the upcoming gap: one step maps to degree 1, two to degree 2, continuing through seven. Decide pitches only from this rule; the synth must not invent a separate melody. Repeat the kick cycle four times, then permute its gap order while preserving the same multiset of distances, producing a new derived note order. Keep harmony, register, tempo, and sound design fixed. End after presenting both maps together once, with the FM voice above the original kick order and bass accents below the permutation. Avoid chords, solos, risers, fills, and random notes. Use wholly original material; do not imitate or reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature rhythm, recording, or sample.
```
