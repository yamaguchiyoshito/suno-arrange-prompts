```text
Create instrumental acid IDM on one fixed tempo grid. Start with dry kick, clipped snare, and an original syncopated synth-bass cell. Add a second layer implying double speed through short hats, rim clicks, and broken sixteenth-note gaps. Keep both layers active, but foreground only one.

Every eight bars, exchange their depth, brightness, and width in a hard two-beat fold without changing tempo. Mutate one bass property per cycle—filter, note length, accent, or octave—while its rhythm stays fixed. Mark exchanges with brief silence. Avoid pads, risers, four-on-the-floor drops, and random fills. End with both layers equally audible for one cycle, then cut on a missing downbeat. Use original notes and patterns; never reproduce an existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
