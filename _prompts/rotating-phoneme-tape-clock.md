```text
Create a lo-fi instrumental clockwork piece from twelve newly recorded neutral phonemes. Chop each to a dry percussion hit; use no words, lead vocal, or sung phrase. Arrange the set as an uneven one-bar loop above a soft analog chord and sparse broken beat.

Every sixteen bars, rotate only the loop's starting point forward by one slice; keep its internal spacing and the chord pulse fixed. Give one slice a bright locator tone and never alter it. Add mild wow, flutter, hiss, and softened attacks without hiding the timing. Introduce no new melody after the opening. When the locator returns to the downbeat, remove the drums, run one final cycle against the pad, and stop mid-hiss. Use only original recordings and notes; never reproduce an existing melody, lyric, signature sequence, recording, or recognizable sample.
```
