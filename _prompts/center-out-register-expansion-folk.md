```text
Create restrained chamber folk for lead vocal, fingerpicked guitar, bowed bass, and one soft string voice. Begin with every part confined to the same central octave. Keep the number of simultaneous notes, tempo, and master loudness stable throughout.

Every eight bars, transpose exactly one existing line up an octave and a different existing line down an octave; remove both original-register versions, so expansion never adds layers. Let the vocal remain central until the third expansion, then move one wordless harmony upward while the lead stays close. A dry snare may strike once only at each register boundary. Reach maximum width without playing louder or denser. End on the widest voicing with the center briefly empty. Compose original music and lyrics only; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, chord sequence, arrangement signature, recording, or sample.
```
