```text
Create minimalist spoken-word music from one processed vocal syllable repeated at an exact interval. It is the only percussion; keep its pitch, length, volume, and position fixed.

Place calm speech around the loop. Alternate intimate dry lines with processed low responses. Add only thin organ and a distant synth chord, each held for many pulses. Leave silence between sentences and move harmony slowly.

Do not add drums, bass groove, arpeggios, sung chorus, cinematic rise, or resolution. Build through language density, stereo placement, and distance between dry and processed voices. Near the end, overlap two spoken fragments without forming harmony. Remove organ and responses, leave the original syllable alone for eight cycles, then cut it off mid-pattern.
```
