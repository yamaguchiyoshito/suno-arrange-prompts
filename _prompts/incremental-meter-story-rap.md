Create abstract story-rap whose bars grow while delivery speed stays exact. Write an original narrative in concrete sentences. Repeat a dry three-hit machine cell with fixed sound, spacing, and tempo. Begin in three-beat bars, then move to four, five, and six beats, holding each for eight bars. Place every added beat after the fixed cell. Leave it silent on first pass, then add one small noise without changing the original hits. Keep syllable rate constant and extend sentences with new words, never stretched vowels or filler pauses. Build pressure through denser language and closer vocal distance, not louder drums. Mark each meter change with one bar of voice alone. Stop after the first complete six-beat sentence. Avoid hooks, sung choruses, melodic samples, borrowed lyrics, swing, fills, risers, and recognizable source narratives.
