Create vocal electro-funk hip-hop at 94 BPM with an original rubbery synth bass, dry drum machine, sparse whistle lead, and contrasting rap and sung hook. The resulting scene route must be A-B-B-C-C-C-A, with hard cuts and no unannounced transition. Keep each scene's internal orchestration identical whenever it repeats. Use newly created melody, harmony, lyrics, performances, recordings, and sounds only.
