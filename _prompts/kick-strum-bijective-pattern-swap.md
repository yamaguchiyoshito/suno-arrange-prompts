```text
Create live-band vocal abstract hip-hop at 102 BPM with two contrasting voices, bass guitar, dry drums, and clipped electric-guitar strums. Write separate two-bar patterns for kick and guitar with equal hit counts and no shared sixteenth-note positions. Keep snare, hats, bass, harmony, vocals, and dynamics fixed.

Before playback, pair each kick position one-to-one with one guitar position in a fixed order. Every eight bars, exchange the next unused pair of grid positions and permanently lock that pair. Never exchange a position twice, move any other event, create overlaps, or change either pattern's hit count. Continue until every original kick position belongs to guitar and every original guitar position belongs to kick. End in the fully exchanged state; do not reset or combine the patterns. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
