```text
Create hazy shoegaze around a continuous wall of distorted guitars.
Layer guitars with slightly different tunings, inversions, tones, and attack positions. Let pitch edges rub gently; never align every layer perfectly.
Use bending chord shapes, tremolo-arm motion, reverse swells, and softened pick attack while keeping the harmony readable beneath the blur.
Bass follows roots with rounded distortion. Drums hold a steady pulse: soft kick, broad snare, and cymbals merging into the guitars.
Vocal: quiet, breathy, restrained, and embedded inside the guitar spectrum. Add one low chorus double, never a choir.
Use a nearly hidden analog pad only between guitar tails. Widen the chorus without raising its volume.
No guitar solo, virtuoso lead, sharp transient, or polished separation. End on a sustained chord whose tuning slowly drifts apart.
```
