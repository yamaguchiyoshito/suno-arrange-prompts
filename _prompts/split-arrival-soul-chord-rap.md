Create sparse vocal abstract hip-hop at 76 BPM with dry drums, rounded sub bass, and a four-voice electric-piano chord. Use original harmony and a calm rap performance. Split every chord attack into three arrival times: the lowest electric-piano voice enters one sixteenth before the beat, the two middle voices land exactly on the beat, and the highest voice enters one sixteenth after it. Sustain the voices so they fuse after arrival; do not arpeggiate further.

Keep this timing for bars 1–8. For bars 9–16, reverse only the outer arrivals: highest voice early, middle voices on-grid, lowest voice late. Repeat the first state in bars 17–24 and the reversed state in bars 25–32, then end on one unsplit chord. Preserve pitches, voicing, note count, velocity, drums, and vocal rhythm. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
