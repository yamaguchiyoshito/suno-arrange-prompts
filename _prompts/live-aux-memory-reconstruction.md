```text
Create vocal live dub at 78 BPM with bass, drums, guitar, organ, and audible room ambience. Perform a complete original 16-bar closing section while separately capturing four auxiliary memories: bass below 120 Hz, room microphones, tape-delay return, and vocal-reverb return.

At the downbeat after bar 16, cut every direct instrument and forbid all new playing. Build a 32-bar coda only from the captured returns. Bars 1–8 use the isolated low-frequency memory; bars 9–16 add room memory; bars 17–24 replace low memory with tape delay; bars 25–32 use only room plus vocal reverb. Never restore dry signal or trigger a fresh note. Keep return gains fixed and let natural decay determine the ending, with the last eight bars fading only because the stored energy runs out. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
