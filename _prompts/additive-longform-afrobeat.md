```text
Build long-form Afrobeat over unbroken 4/4. Start with dry kick, snare, and shaker; keep tempo and harmony fixed. Add one layer every sixteen bars: melodic bass, muted guitar A, contrasting guitar B, hand percussion, clipped electric keys, then compact horns. Each repeats one figure without fills.

Keep guitars in separate registers with gaps between them. Let conga and shaker cross the beat while drums hold the meter. Keys give percussive answers, never pads. At full density, horns play brief unison calls separated by silence.

Avoid chord-heavy verses, drops, solos, and constant horns. Hold the full ensemble until the composite groove becomes the event. Then subtract one layer every sixteen bars in reverse. Leave bass, drums, and one guitar, then stop together on beat one without cadence.
```
