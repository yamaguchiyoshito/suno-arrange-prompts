```text
Create dark instrumental abstract hip-hop with a slow dry break, deep mono bass, vinyl dust, and isolated instrumental fragments. Build an original drum cell from a dull kick, cracked snare, closed hat, and one ghost hit. Keep the main snare fixed; every four bars, swap the positions of only two secondary hits. Let the bass sustain one pitch for several bars and change only at section boundaries. Insert newly recorded single piano notes and breathy horn tones as distant events, never as chords or a lead melody. After each fragment, leave at least one beat completely empty. Twice in the track, mute everything for half a bar while the vinyl texture continues. Avoid vocals, lush pads, constant crackle, jazz soloing, bright cymbals, and a rising climax. End with the break removed, one bass pulse, then room noise cut to silence.
```
