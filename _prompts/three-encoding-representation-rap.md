```text
Choose one original sixteen-step binary rhythm at 92 BPM containing exactly six hits. Represent it in three deterministic ways. Drums play the six binary hit positions directly. Bass plays one note per silent run, with note duration equal to that run's length. A sampler plays at every drum hit, with pitch degree equal to the number of steps until the next hit, wrapping at step sixteen. Present eight bars of drums alone, eight of bass alone, eight of sampler alone, then sixteen bars combining all three encodings under an original rap. Never alter the source bit pattern; any change in one representation must be recalculated from the same pattern. Use no unrelated fills or countermelodies. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
