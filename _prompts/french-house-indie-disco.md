```text
Tight indie-disco arrangement with French-house polish and dry rhythmic guitars.

Keep the bright melodic lift, but make the track leaner, sharper, and more danceable. Build it around clipped funk guitar stabs, rubbery syncopated bass, glassy synth arpeggios, and a compact electronic drum groove.

Drums should be dry and close: short kick, crisp snare, tight 16th hats, small claps, and filtered shakers. Avoid big rock room sound or heavy EDM low end.

Guitars act as percussion, not a wall: clean muted upstrokes, short chord hits, high-passed upper mids, slightly off-center. Add a thinner double only in the chorus.

Vocals stay cool, close, and slightly detached, with stacked doubles on chorus hooks. Add crowd-like harmony only at phrase endings.

Structure: clipped intro → taut verse → glossy chorus → filtered breakdown → final chorus with octave synths and brighter percussion.

Make it feel stylish, nervous, dry, and expensive.
```
