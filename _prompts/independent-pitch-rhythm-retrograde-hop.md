Create vocal abstract hip-hop at 86 BPM with dry drums, steady synth bass, electric piano, and measured rap. Write one original eight-note phrase and record its pitch sequence and its eight inter-onset intervals, including rests and the final wrap to the next repetition, separately. Present four eight-bar states: both sequences forward; pitches backward with intervals forward; pitches forward with intervals backward; then both sequences backward.

Keep note count, tonal center, voicing, drum pattern, bass, velocity, and phrase length fixed. Do not add transitions or variations between states. After the fourth state, play only the first two notes of the original forward phrase and cut before the third, without resolving. Make the music wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
