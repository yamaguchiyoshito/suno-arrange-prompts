```text
Create precise micro-edit pop where stereo position, silence, and re-entry compose the form. Use clean guitar figures, clipped vocal syllables, hand taps, clicks, room sounds, and compact bass. Keep lead clear; use breaths and consonants rhythmically without lyric repeats.

Keep bass, kick, and lead centered. Place guitar/found fragments hard left/right, near center, or moving. Pass a motif left-to-right note by note, answer with opposite-moving texture, collapse to mono for one beat, then reopen.

Build a minimal dry beat from tiny acoustic/electronic attacks. Interrupt exactly: mute one layer for an eighth; stop all for a quarter; resume from the opposite channel; restart one subdivision early. Avoid random glitches, dense fills, constant widening, and long reverbs. Recombine limited sounds instead of adding chorus layers. Speed channel exchanges and shorten silences while the center stays stable. End with one sound crossing stereo, then silence.
```
