```text
Create slowcore in two unequal halves joined by one hard splice. First, use only close acoustic guitar and two restrained voices singing wholly original words in narrow harmony. Leave long gaps after each line; expose the guitar pattern, chord order, and room tone. Midway through a sustained syllable, cut with no pause, swell, count-in, or transition to a full-volume wall of doubled electric-guitar fuzz. Preserve the harmony, melodic contour, vocal register, and slow pulse so only scale, density, and texture change. Continue the interrupted syllable through the splice, now nearly buried, while drums remain implied. Never return to the quiet arrangement or reduce saturation. Cut the loud texture in mid-chord. Avoid solos, feedback theatrics, tempo changes, new sections, borrowed lyrics, and recognizable melodies.
```
