Create cinematic vocal pop that changes its sense of time at one boundary. First, lock a single-note sequence, analog pad, and close vocal to a precise pulse. Use original harmony, melody, and lyrics; keep the singer in a narrow middle register and begin each phrase on the same two pulse positions. At the midpoint, cut the sequence with no fade or transitional sound. Continue the vocal in the same register and intensity, but enter free time. Replace the grid with strings that enter only at breaths, overlap in long unequal arcs, and avoid repeated figures. Widen their range around the vocal center without restoring a pulse. Let the final line finish alone, followed by one unresolved string harmonic. Avoid drums, arpeggios after the cut, tempo accents, key changes, climactic percussion, borrowed lyrics, and recognizable melodies.
