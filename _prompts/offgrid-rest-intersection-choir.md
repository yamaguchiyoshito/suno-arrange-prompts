```text
Create progressive folk from three original repeating figures lasting five, seven, and nine eighth notes. Assign them to muted electric guitar, low plucked guitar, and hand percussion. Give each loop at least one rest and let all three run continuously without reset, quantization, or phase correction.

A small choir may enter only when rests in all three loops coincide. At every such intersection, sing one short new word, then permanently remove one loop immediately afterward. Keep the remaining loops cycling at their original lengths, making each later choir entrance arise from a different texture. After the third intersection, leave only unaccompanied voices sustaining the last word's vowels, then cut them before resolution. Use entirely original music and lyrics; never copy any existing melody, lyric, rhythmic signature, ensemble gesture, recording, or sample.
```
