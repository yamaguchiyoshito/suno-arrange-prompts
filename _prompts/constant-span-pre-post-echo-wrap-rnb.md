```text
Create an original slow R&B song at 88 BPM with one exposed electric-piano chord on every second beat. Surround each chord with one pre-echo and one post-echo at equal -9 dB gain. Across five eight-bar sections, use the offset pairs 0 and 1 beat, 1/4 and 3/4 beat, 1/2 and 1/2 beat, 3/4 and 1/4 beat, then 1 and 0 beat. The pre-offset plus post-offset must always equal exactly one beat; treat zero offset as a layer on the dry chord. Keep chord onset, harmony, voice, drums, bass, echo tone, and total gain fixed. Use no other delay or reverb, and wrap pre-echoes across the preceding bar when necessary. End with the symmetric 1/2-and-1/2 state once more. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
