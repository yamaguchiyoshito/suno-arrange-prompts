```text
Create a beatless orchestral sound mass with no lead melody, repeating motif, or meter. Divide strings, woodwinds, and brass into quiet individual lines. Each sustains a pitch, then moves by a neighboring semitone or whole tone at an independent rate; avoid synchronized attacks and recognizable chord changes. Begin as a thin low layer of basses, cellos, bassoons, and muted brass with wide internal gaps. Gradually add adjacent tones and higher instruments until a dense cloud fills the register, using small sectional swells without pulse. At maximum density, remove low voices first, then middle voices, leaving violins, flutes, and faint upper harmonics alone. Use no percussion, piano, solos, ostinatos, tonal cadences, or impact hits. Keep every transition continuous. End with high tones fading at different rates, unresolved.
```
