Create an original instrumental ambient-bossa piece at 104 BPM. Use fingerpicked nylon-string guitar, a round synth bass, soft shaker, and quiet rim taps in a gently syncopated groove. Write a guitar part with a small upper melody above a repeating inner-voice chord pattern. Open with four bars of guitar, then play four eight-bar sections. In sections one and three, include both the upper melody and inner chords. In sections two and four, omit only the upper melody while keeping every inner-voice attack, bass note, and percussion hit unchanged. Preserve the same tempo and quiet dynamics through these changes. Finish with a four-bar coda restoring the complete guitar part as the percussion fades. Warm open-air color, clear finger noise, modest room reverb, and a light low-end pulse. Let the small changes in register provide movement without adding instruments. All melody, harmony, and recordings must be original.
