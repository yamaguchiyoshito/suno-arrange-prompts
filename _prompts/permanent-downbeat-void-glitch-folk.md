```text
Make an original glitch-folk song in 4/4 with lead vocal, clipped electric guitar, viola, and tiny electronic percussion. Reserve beat one of every bar as absolute silence: no attack, sustain, reverb tail, breath, or delay may cross it.

Begin with sounds on only two sixteenth-note positions outside beat one. Every eight bars, activate exactly two more positions and distribute them among guitar, viola, voice fragments, and percussion. Once activated, a position may change timbre but not timing. Build urgency by filling the permitted grid while preserving the recurring black hole at each downbeat. Before the ending, remove active positions in pairs until only one offbeat guitar note remains. Follow it with an empty final bar. Write wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, rhythmic signature, sound design, recording, or sample.
```
