```text
Create instrumental IDM at 104 BPM from four new recordings: water drip, metal strike, footstep, and bowed scrape. Shape each to the same attack, duration, loudness envelope, and narrow pitch range. Assign them to fixed positions in one broken two-bar rhythm. Begin with water in every position. Every eight bars, replace exactly one position with the next material while preserving timing, contour, velocity, and silence; never layer old and new. At the midpoint, reverse stereo locations without changing the pattern. Continue until every position has changed, then cut before any state repeats. Keep the mix dry and close. Avoid chords, pads, risers, fills, random edits, and added drums. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
