```text
Create an 88 BPM live-band vocal rap track with bass guitar, piano, horn, and acoustic drums. Write a repeating four-bar band phrase in which all four roles have exactly one short attack on the following downbeat. At each four-bar boundary, move one role's next downbeat attack exactly one eighth note earlier across the barline; its original beat-one position becomes a complete rest. Rotate bass→piano→horn→drums. Never displace within a bar, add a replacement, sustain, or echo. Keep event counts, pitches, dynamics, timbres, and other positions fixed. Let an original rap vocal cross each boundary unchanged. After two rotations, play the last borrowed drum hit, continue through the empty drum downbeat while the other roles sound, and stop before beat two. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
