```text
Create an original 76 BPM rap track controlled by three binary switches: X selects low or high kick register, Y selects short or sustained bass, and Z selects mono or wide chord voicing. Present eight eight-bar sections in this exact three-bit Gray-code order: 000, 001, 011, 010, 110, 111, 101, 100. Exactly one switch must change at every boundary. Keep the two-bar onset pattern, pitches, vocal cadence, velocities, and overall loudness identical across all states. Announce each change with no fill; the new state begins directly on the next downbeat. End after state 100 without resolving to 000, and never combine both alternatives of a switch. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
