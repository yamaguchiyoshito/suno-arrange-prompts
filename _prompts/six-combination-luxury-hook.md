```text
Create polished vocal electro-funk at 98 BPM with deep synth bass, crisp drums, original rap verses, and a melodic hook. For every 12-bar hook, schedule one lead entrance on beat one of bars 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11. Build the lead from exactly three timbres—pure sine whistle, muted brass, and synthetic choir—and exactly two articulations—an eighth-note stab and a two-beat sustain.

Use all six timbre-articulation pairs once and only once in this order: sine stab, brass sustain, choir stab, sine sustain, brass stab, choir sustain. Each entrance plays the same newly written two-note interval; no two lead timbres may overlap, and the lead must remain silent outside those six entrances. Keep verses lean so the combinatorial hook reads clearly. All melody, harmony, lyrics, performances, recordings, and synthesis must be original. Do not imitate DJ PMX, the referenced song, its performers, recognizable riffs, flows, recording, or samples.
```
