```text
Forward 16th-note groove with slightly loose quantization.
Drums: tight kick, light snare, constant hats; add subtle electronic percussion layers.

Bass is melodic (8ths with leaps), lightly sidechained to the kick.
Gtr1: clean/light drive for cutting and hooky single-note lines.
Gtr2: chorus/delay textures, looping motifs.
Keys: digital pads + arpeggiator filling upper mids.

Harmony: major base with add9/11; lift the chorus with brief modulation or substitutions.
Vocal: close and clear; doubles only in chorus. Sparse synth countermelodies.

Build in 8-bar layers. Mix: tight lows, clean 200–400 Hz; wide guitars/keys, vocal centered; short plate + delay for clarity. Fast, transparent feel.
```
