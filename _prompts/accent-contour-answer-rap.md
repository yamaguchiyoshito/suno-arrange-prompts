```text
Create an upbeat broken-beat rap song using EVISBEATS' “Choudo Yeah!” only as a high-level point of departure. Construct every four-bar unit from one newly written one-bar vocal phrase containing exactly four accented syllables. Bar 1 presents the phrase over a minimal beat. In bar 2, remove the lead voice and place a drum fill at exactly those four accent positions. In bar 3, remove the fill and let four short bass notes occupy the same positions, preserving the vocal phrase's relative accent strengths as note velocities. In bar 4, leave those four positions completely silent while the rest of the groove continues. Repeat the rule with new words and new accent positions; never overlap call and answer. Finish with one unit in which only the four silences remain. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate EVISBEATS or the source track.
```
