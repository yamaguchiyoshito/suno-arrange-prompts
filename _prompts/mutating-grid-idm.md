```text
Create midtempo IDM from a rigid sixteen-step grid. Start with dry kick, short snare, two metallic ticks, and compact synth-bass. Establish the full bar first; keep tempo and grid audible.

Every four bars, alter one event only: move a tick by one step, shorten a hit, change a timbre, or reverse a tiny tail. Never change two properties together. Accumulate edits for four cycles, then restore one earlier state while another part advances. Pan sounds but keep kick and bass centered.

Avoid fast jungle breaks, random glitches, chord progressions, pads, risers, drops, and vocals. Use no melody beyond the bass cell. Keep loudness even; complexity must emerge from traceable mutations in the same grid. Finally remove events in their entry order until one off-center tick remains, then end after an incomplete bar.
```
