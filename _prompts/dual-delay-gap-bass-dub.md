```text
Create extremely slow vocal dub at 70 BPM with a dark organ stab, deep electric bass, sparse drum kit, and soft lead voice. Send each organ stab into two separate delays: one repeats after three eighth notes and the other after five eighth notes, with one repeat each and no feedback.

Across a repeating two-bar grid, place bass notes only on eighth-note positions untouched by the dry organ or either delayed repeat. Place kick, snare, and closed hi-hat only on positions where neither organ echoes nor bass sounds. Never allow the three rhythmic layers to strike together. Keep the vocal free above the grid but cut its reverb whenever bass enters. Every 16 bars, remove one original organ stab while preserving both delay times, making the open spaces wider. End with bass alone in the final available gap. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
