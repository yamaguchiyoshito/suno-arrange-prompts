```text
Create theatrical dark pop in strict three-beat waltz. Lock a brittle music-box figure, dry accordion chords, and short pizzicato strings into one repeating mechanism. Keep tempo, meter, and main harmony unchanged.

Use an unnaturally close lead vocal, controlled and childlike first, then insistent through whispered doubles. After each section add one disturbance only: a chromatic countermelody, octave overtone, or pizzicato answer shifted within the bar. Never replace the original parts.

Double note density while the pulse stays fixed; crowd high registers until toy-like clarity turns anxious. Avoid unrelated key changes, rock guitars, cinematic swells, and EDM drops. At the peak, cut all added layers together. Leave music box and accordion for one bar, then stop the mechanism on beat two.
```
