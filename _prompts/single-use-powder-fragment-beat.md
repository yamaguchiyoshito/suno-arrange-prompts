```text
Make a soft 74 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop miniature with a broken low-impact beat and large white gaps. Prepare exactly 12 unique foreground fragments: four powdery harp notes, four muted vibraphone notes, and four tiny wing-like found sounds. Number them 1–12 before arranging.

Across four successive four-bar sections, introduce exactly three previously unused fragments per section. Each numbered fragment may sound once in the entire track—no loop, echo, pitched copy, reversal, or later reprise. A quiet two-note sub pulse and the same drum skeleton may continue underneath, but never fill the gaps left by spent fragments. After fragment 12, leave two empty bars containing only room noise. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
