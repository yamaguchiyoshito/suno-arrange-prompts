```text
Warm indie-folk arrangement with a soft, nostalgic pulse.

Start with fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a muted upright piano playing simple suspended chords. Keep the tempo relaxed, slightly behind the beat. Add a round, melodic bass that moves gently, never dominating.

Drums should be brushed and restrained: soft kick, light snare, tambourine only in the chorus. Use a dry, intimate kit sound rather than big 80s reverb.

Vocals stay close and human, with a fragile lead and subtle doubles on key lines. Add small three-part harmonies in the chorus, soft and blended, not theatrical.

Introduce pedal steel or clean electric guitar swells after verse lines, like distant light. Use warm tape saturation, short plate reverb, and a wide but uncluttered mix.

Structure: quiet verse → lifted chorus → stripped bridge → final chorus with gentle communal harmonies.
```
