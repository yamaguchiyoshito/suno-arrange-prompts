```text
Create an original sparse Japanese rap track at 78 BPM in 4/4. Use a dark analog pad, deep centered sub bass, short drum-machine kicks, a dry snare, and widely spaced hats. Keep the lead in a comfortable low-to-mid register, close and intelligible, with steady projection and room for each phrase to land. Build four eight-bar sections, alternating dense narrative verses and concise rapped refrains. At beats three and four of every eighth bar, mute all instruments and their effect tails; place the final words of the rap phrase in that two-beat gap. Bring the beat back on the next downbeat, except after the fourth section, where the exposed voice ends the song. Keep the vocal dry during each gap. Let tension come from the contrast between sub weight and bare speech. Use small pad voicing changes without adding extra percussion. All harmony, rhythms, lyrics, vocal phrasing, and sound material must be newly created.
```
