```text
Create transparent math rock with original musical material. Keep a tonal center through open strings, suspensions, and incomplete chords. Over steady 4/4, interlock a five-note clean-guitar arpeggio crossing bars and a three-note high reply, with drifting accents. Warm bass adds anchor notes.

Live drums drive motion: dry hi-hat, controlled kick, clear backbeat, snare ghosts, displaced rims, and brief tom replies. Preserve human timing and velocity. Avoid hard quantization, double kick, huge cymbal washes, and decorative fills.

Start with guitar one and ghost notes; add guitar two, bass, and vocal without changing pulse. Build via denser interlocks and stronger drums, never distortion or orchestra. At the peak, briefly align cycles, bass, snare, and vocal. Then drop bass and full snare, leaving guitar one and faint ghosts. Stop drums; end on ringing open tones. Mix airy with short natural room, minimal compression, and clear detail.
```
