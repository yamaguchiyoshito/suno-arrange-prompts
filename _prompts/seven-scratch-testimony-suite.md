```text
Create a 90 BPM original vocal hip-hop suite with seven four-bar rap statements over one dry beat. Record seven original one-word interjections and assign them one-to-one to these scratch gestures: baby, chirp, stab, transform, tear, orbit, and drag. Place exactly one unique scratched word between consecutive statements, then use the seventh after the final statement. Never repeat a word, gesture, or scratch recording, and do not use a chorus. Keep the backing groove unchanged so the seven cuts define the form. Use wholly original lyrics, voices, music, and recordings; do not sample or imitate any existing performer.
```
