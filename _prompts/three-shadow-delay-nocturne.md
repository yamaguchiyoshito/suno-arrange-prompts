```text
Create a 64 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop nocturne with a low worn break, a two-note organ figure, a deep single-note bass anchor, and rare muted-horn fragments. Leave at least one full beat of silence between foreground events. Send each organ note and horn fragment into three discrete tape echoes delayed by exactly 3, 5, and 7 eighth notes.

Keep the dry source centered; place the 3-eighth echo left, the 5-eighth echo right, and the 7-eighth echo narrowly centered. Make each successive echo darker and quieter, with no feedback beyond those three repeats. Every eight bars, mute the dry source for two bars so only the three timing shadows describe the motif. Finish on the seventh-eighth shadow alone. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
