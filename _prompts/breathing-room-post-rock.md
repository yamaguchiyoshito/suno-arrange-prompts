```text
Create spacious post-rock around faint organ and a real room. Begin with organ alone, with key noise and natural decay. Let each player introduce one phrase: brushed cymbal, low drum answer, clean guitar harmonic, then a restrained vocal line.

Leave room tone after gestures. Parts answer without forming a loop; before the peak, no player repeats or re-enters. Keep the vocal fragile and distant. At one central peak, all players return once on a forceful chord made from earlier pitches, then fall to organ and room sound.

Avoid click-tight timing, constant drums, tremolo crescendos, strings, and stacked distortion. Preserve leakage, uneven attacks, and hesitation. Before the peak, every entrance makes one other part stop; no layer remains permanently. After the final vocal line, expose the organ decay and one accidental guitar resonance.
```
