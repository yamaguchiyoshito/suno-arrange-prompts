```text
Create a restrained 72 BPM vocal trap piece with one dry drum pattern, a clean sub-bass line, and one four-note analog-pad chord loop. Change only bit depth every eight bars: drum/chord buses use 16/6, 12/8, 8/12, then 6/16 bit. After the fourth state, retrace only the preceding three pairs: 8/12, 12/8, and 16/6. Gain-match every state. Keep note positions, event counts, pitches, durations, stereo width, tempo, sub, and vocal delivery fixed. Use no distortion outside those two buses and add no transition effects. Write sparse original rap phrases that leave the exchange audible. End at the restored 16/6 pair. Use entirely original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
