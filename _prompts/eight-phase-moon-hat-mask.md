```text
Create instrumental electronic hip-hop at 92 BPM in eight four-bar moon phases. Use one identical closed-hat sample on a sixteen-step grid. The active step sets for phases 1-5 are respectively {0,8}, {0,4,8,12}, {0,2,4,6,8,10,12,14}, every step except {1,5,9,13}, and all steps 0-15. Phases 6, 7, and 8 must repeat the masks of phases 4, 3, and 2, producing counts 2,4,8,12,16,12,8,4.

Keep hat velocity, decay, tuning, and sample identity constant; add no open hats or rolls. Hold one original kick-snare pattern, rounded synth bass, and cool FM chords unchanged for all 32 bars so only the mask waxes and wanes. End after phase 8 without returning to phase 1. Use entirely original composition, performance, recordings, and synthesis. Do not imitate Aru-2, Kzyboost, the referenced track, its exact groove, timbres, riffs, recording, or samples.
```
