```text
Create a nocturnal dub song at 76 BPM with intimate lead vocal, deep melodic bass, restrained drums, clipped electric guitar, and dim organ. Keep one imagined empty seat in the mix: only one of vocal, guitar, or organ may occupy a long wet reverb at any time.

Use 24-bar cycles split into three eight-bar blocks. In block one, only the vocal receives a six-second reverb; guitar and organ stay completely dry. In block two, transfer that same reverb to guitar. In block three, transfer it to organ. At each boundary, let only the previous owner's tail survive for no more than two beats; keep the next owner dry until that tail ends, then transfer the wet seat. Bass and drums remain dry, centered, and rhythmically unchanged throughout both cycles. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
