```text
Create instrumental chamber pop at 112 BPM in 4/4. Repeat one original harpsichord cell exactly seven eighth notes long without resetting at bar lines. Against a 4/4 bar of eight eighth notes, its opening shifts by one eighth each bar and realigns after exactly 56 eighth notes: seven bars and eight cell repetitions. Mark only that point with one sustained string chord. Keep harpsichord pitches and rhythm fixed for four cycles; change only string voicing at each realignment: closed, open, inverted, then unison. Sustain a quiet tonal center below. Avoid drums, vocals, rubato, counterpoint, resets, modulation, and cadences. After the fourth marker, stop halfway through the next cell. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
