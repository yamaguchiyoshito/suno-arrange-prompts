Create an original Japanese hip-hop song at 88 BPM. Use dusty swung breakbeats, rounded electric bass, warm Rhodes, and a newly recorded wordless soul phrase. Chop that phrase into short answers between conversational rap lines; reveal it uncut beneath each eight-bar sung hook. Keep the lead relaxed, clear, and slightly behind the drums, with playful pauses and understated melodic endings. Let the supplied lyrics feel like everyday conversation turning quietly reflective. Open with four bars of filtered chords, then alternate sixteen-bar verses and eight-bar hooks. Before the last hook, leave two bars of Rhodes and voice alone. Widen the backing harmonies on the return while keeping the drums compact. Finish with the soul phrase and a short tape tail. Warm mids, crisp snare, light tape grain, intimate lead vocal. Use original melodies, phrasing, performances, and recordings.
