```text
Shape it as a modern nostalgic pop with clear dynamics and air.

Start with a dry electric piano outlining the chords, lightly swung, paired with a muted guitar picking upper extensions (add9/6).
Keep the intro intimate. Verse: minimal drums (rim clicks + soft kick), warm sub bass entering only on downbeats, vocal very forward with short plate reverb.

Pre-chorus: open the harmony—add suspended tones, widen stereo guitars, introduce a soft pad for lift. Chorus: full band—tight kick/snare, sidechained pad, bright but controlled lead guitar counter-melody.
Stack vocals in thirds and fifths for emotional density.

Bridge: strip back to piano + vocal, add strings in slow swells, then re-enter groove with half-time feel.
Use automation to expand reverb and stereo width toward the final chorus.
End with a lingering unresolved extension (add9) to keep the bittersweet tone.
```
