```text
Write wistful blue-toned indie pop at 92 BPM with warm lead vocal, bass, light drums, clean electric guitar, and plain piano. Fix the harmonic rhythm at exactly four short chord attacks per two-bar unit.

Begin with all four attacks on guitar offbeats: the “and” after beats 1 and 3 in each bar. Every eight bars, move exactly one attack from guitar to piano and place the piano hit one eighth note earlier on the preceding downbeat. Delete the corresponding guitar hit, so the total remains four. After 32 bars, piano owns all four onbeats and guitar is silent; reverse the transfer one hit every eight bars without changing chords, tempo, bass line, or drum density. End halfway through the return, with two attacks on each instrument. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
