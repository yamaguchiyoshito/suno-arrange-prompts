Route A through a fourth-order Linkwitz–Riley low-pass filter and B through its matching high-pass filter at one shared crossover frequency. Hold each setting for four bars and move through 250, 500, 1000, 2000, and 4000 Hz, then reverse. Never present either phrase unfiltered. Use short original rap lines separated by exposed gaps. At the final 250 Hz return, mute both phrases and leave only voice, drums, and sub for two bars. Use wholly original melodies, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
