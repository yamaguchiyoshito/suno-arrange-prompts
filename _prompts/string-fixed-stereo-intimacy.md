```text
Create intimate free-time folk for centered lead vocal, one acoustic guitar, and a quiet viola near the center. Treat the six guitar strings as six permanently fixed points: low to high at L100, L60, L20, R20, R60, and R100. Use no autopan, stereo chorus, or moving delay.

Write fingerpicked chords whose notes arrive on separate strings, so harmony becomes a constellation across the six static positions. Avoid broad strums. In each new section, omit one string and redistribute no notes to replace it; let the missing spatial point alter the chord. Keep vocal and viola narrow so they never mask the map. Restore all six strings only for the penultimate chord, then end with the outermost two strings alone. Compose original music and lyrics; never copy any existing melody, lyric, guitar figure, spatial signature, recording, or sample.
```
