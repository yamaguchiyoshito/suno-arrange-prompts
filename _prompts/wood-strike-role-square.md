```text
Create an instrumental broken dub beat using 16FLIP's “Wood Dub” only as a high-level structural reference. Record four distinct original wood strikes, W1 through W4, and assign them to four fixed rhythmic roles: kick pattern, snare pattern, closed-hat pattern, and ghost-note pattern. Every eight bars rotate the recordings one role clockwise while all four role patterns remain unchanged. Do not retune, filter, layer, truncate, reverse, or replace a wood recording; only its rhythmic role may move. Complete four sections so each recording performs each role exactly once. A sub-bass note may strike only with the current kick role, and one delay throw may occur only on the final current-snare hit of a section. After the fourth section, play one bar containing W1 in all four role positions, then cut. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate 16FLIP or the source track.
```
