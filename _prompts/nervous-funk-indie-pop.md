A tight, nervous indie-pop arrangement built on clipped funk guitar, rubbery bass, and a dry, danceable drum groove.
The guitars avoid big chords, working as percussive hooks with bright chorus and palm-muted sixteenth notes.
The bass carries the song’s swagger, moving melodically while locking hard with the kick.
Drums are crisp and minimal: snappy snare, closed hi-hats, little room sound, almost programmed in feel.
Synths and guitar layers add glossy 80s color without overcrowding the mix.
The vocal sits intimate and conversational, slightly detached, letting the rhythm do most of the emotional work.
The key move is restraint: everything feels casual, but every part is rhythmically precise.
