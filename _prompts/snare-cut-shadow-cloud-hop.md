Create a 66 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop piece inside a dark, nearly empty stereo field. Use a muffled four-bar break, one deep sustained bass note per two bars, low string smears, isolated reversed tonal clouds, and a distant electronic signal. Keep every element separated by exposed silence; never let more than three sound sources overlap.

Build four eight-bar sections. At the final snare of bars 8, 16, 24, and 32, hard-cut every reverb and delay return to absolute silence while leaving the dry snare intact. Begin the next bar with only bass, then restore one atmospheric element every two bars. Let the last cut end the track with no tail. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
