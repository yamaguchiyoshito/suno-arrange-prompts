```text
Create an original soulful Japanese rap track at 92 BPM. Use warm sustained organ chords, a rounded electric bass, dry drums with a gentle swing, and small guitar accents. Keep the rap delivery calm, direct, and rhythmically firm, with natural breaths and clear words. Let the supplied lyrics feel reflective and quietly grateful. Build a four-bar intro, a sixteen-bar verse, an eight-bar hook, a second verse and hook, a four-bar organ-and-bass bridge, and a final hook. In each hook, keep the rapper in front and place a brief sung answer after every four-bar rap phrase. Introduce soft horn voicings only in the final hook, filling the vocal gaps without doubling the lead melody. Keep earlier hooks free of horns so the last return gains warmth. End with organ and bass holding one chord together. Intimate lead, modest room ambience, and a steady low-end pulse. Compose fresh harmony, melodies, phrasing, and recordings.
```
