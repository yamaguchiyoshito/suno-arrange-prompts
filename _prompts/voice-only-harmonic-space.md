```text
Create an unmetered voices-only arrangement. No instruments, samples, beatboxing, or mouth percussion. Use an intimate lead and ensemble: sustained low floor, two middle voices forming close suspensions, and one rare high overtone. Keep parts singable and non-synthetic.

Use free time with no pulse, groove, or fixed bars. Cue entrances from the lead's breaths. Let voices enter apart, then settle into chords. Build tension with seconds, fourths, held tones, and one inner semitone move; resolve selectively, leaving suspensions open.

Keep the lead restrained and clear. Backing voices use vowels and quiet consonants without masking lyrics. Correct pitch only to unify long tones; preserve breath, onset variety, and slight drift. Mix close and centered with stereo width, minimal compression, and short dark ambience. Insert silence between two sections. End on an unresolved cluster, removing voices one by one until only breath remains.
```
