```text
Build a nostalgic, wind-swept atmosphere driven by motion and space.
Start with a clean acoustic guitar or harp articulating the main motif in a flowing arpeggio, lightly humanized timing. Layer a soft pad (analog or airy choir) to establish horizon depth.
Introduce a lead (flute or glassy synth) with gentle vibrato, phrased legato.
Harmony stays diatonic but enrich with add9 and occasional IV→iv color for wistfulness.
Bass enters late, simple root–5th movement, warm and rounded.
Percussion is minimal: brushed snare, light shaker, no hard transients.
Mid-section adds strings in parallel motion, widening stereo.
Automate reverb to grow subtly. End unresolved, letting the final chord breathe into silence.
```
