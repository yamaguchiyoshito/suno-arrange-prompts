```text
Create a 90 BPM instrumental broken-beat hip-hop track. Build a six-piece original kit: kick, snare, closed hat, clap, tom, and cymbal. Start with every sample truncated to exactly 60 ms, producing six unnaturally wounded hits. Every eight bars, replace one truncated hit with a fresh recording of the same instrument and onset but its full natural release, in this order: kick, snare, hat, clap, tom, cymbal. Keep MIDI positions, pitch, peak level, and all other layers unchanged. End as soon as the sixth tail is restored. Use only original recordings and composition; do not reproduce any existing beat, sample, riff, or voice.
```
