Create beatless ambient at a 60 BPM reference. Repeat one original six-note piano chord every eight beats with fixed pitches, register, velocity, and duration. Across five 16-bar stages, set fade-in to 0, 100, 300, 700, then 1500 milliseconds, erasing only the attack. Lengthen room decay and reveal upper overtones with gentle saturation, but add no notes or chord changes. A quiet analog haze follows the same envelope and never precedes the piano. Preserve eight-beat spacing even after onsets blur. Avoid percussion, pulse accents, bass movement, melody, arpeggios, voices, crescendos, and hard edits. After four final repetitions, stop retriggering and let resonance decay. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
