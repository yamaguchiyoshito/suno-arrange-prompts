```text
Create a 104 BPM vocal Afro-pop track on a strict sixteen-step bar. Put only shaker, rim click, and breathy hand percussion on quarter-note downbeats: steps 1, 5, 9, and 13. Place every low-tom and synth-bass attack only on steps 2, 6, 10, and 14; never let either land on a downbeat. Give the light layer an unchanging one-bar loop and alternate bass notes without changing their positions. Add clipped clean guitar between those attacks and an original, relaxed vocal that floats across the grid without doubling the bass. Build by exposing light percussion first, then low tom, bass, guitar, and voice. Remove them in reverse order, ending with one dry shaker downbeat. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; do not imitate any identifiable song, riff, arrangement, or vocal likeness.
```
