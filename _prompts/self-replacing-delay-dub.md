```text
Create sparse instrumental chamber dub at 82 BPM. Begin with an original dry four-note phrase on plucked low strings, centered, plus muted hand drum on beats one and three. Apply a dotted-eighth delay with four repeats. After every fourth phrase, make the final repeat the new dry lead and erase the prior lead; echo replaces its source rather than accompanying it. Do this four times. Each generation narrows bandwidth, shortens attacks, moves clockwise in stereo, and lowers the drum one dynamic step. Keep pitch order, tempo, and event count fixed. Add no chords, bass line, new melody, fill, vocal, or fresh phrase. On generation five, mute the drum and stop delay input; end when the final echo dies. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
