```text
Create experimental vocal pop at 96 BPM. Write an original sixteen-bar first half for close voice, electric piano, bass guitar, and dry drums. Record its complete onset map: the exact position, rest, and duration of every event. At bar seventeen, make one hard cut and rebuild all sixteen bars from that timing map alone. Preserve every onset, rest, duration, and total event count, but replace all pitches, chords, melodic contours, registers, instrumental timbres, and vocal syllables. No pitch or chord from the first half may recur in the same role during the rebuild. Keep tempo and section length fixed so the rhythmic identity survives while the musical surface becomes unrelated. End by playing two bars of the first sound set and two bars of the rebuilt set, sharing only timing. Avoid fills, transitions, extra layers, and quotation. Use original lyrics and music only; do not imitate or reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature rhythm, vocal identity, recording, or sample.
```
