Create slow instrumental ambient music from synthetic choir and organ. Compose a new four-voice harmonic path, omitting its highest voice for the first seventy-five percent. Present bass and two inner lines as long breaths with gaps; keep the upper register empty, including reverb.

Let the three voices change at different rates so suspensions emerge without a pulse. Lengthen releases and widen inner voices without adding notes. In the final quarter, introduce the top line one note at a time until the full sonority appears once. Then remove the organ and let the choir drift into an unresolved three-note remainder. Avoid drums, arpeggios, solo melody, words, swells, and impacts. Use original harmony and voicing; never reproduce an existing melody, lyric, signature progression, recording, choir identity, or sample.
