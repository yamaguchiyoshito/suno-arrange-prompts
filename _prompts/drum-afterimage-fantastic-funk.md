```text
Create a 104 BPM instrumental electro-funk groove from an original dry drum performance and three synthesized afterimages. After each kick has fully ended, allow one low sine tail; after each snare, one short noise chord; after each hat, one FM spark. An afterimage may never overlap the dry hit that caused it or trigger another event. Keep the acoustic drum MIDI fixed for 24 bars. In the final eight bars mute the dry kit while preserving every afterimage at its original delayed position, so the absent groove remains legible. Use wholly original rhythms, synthesis, and harmony; do not imitate an existing record.
```
