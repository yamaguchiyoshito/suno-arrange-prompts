```text
Create a 94 BPM original vocal hip-hop track that travels backward through production tools in four eight-bar stages. Stage one uses the full clean mix. Stage two removes only stereo widening. Stage three also removes the synthesized sub. Stage four also disables time-stretching, and the final stage removes quantization to reveal the raw mono live break and untouched original instrument phrase. Once a tool is removed it may never return; do not add vinyl noise or distortion as shorthand for age. Keep the same original rap and harmony throughout. Do not copy any existing sample, melody, lyrics, cadence, or performer identity.
```
