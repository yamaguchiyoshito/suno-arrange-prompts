```text
Make a smoky 69 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop track with a muffled laid-back break, warm one-note electric-piano replies, rounded mono bass, and two original subway-like field textures: ventilation air and low rail hum. Treat every interval between consecutive drum hits as a separate compartment.

Fill the first compartment with ventilation, the next with rail hum, then alternate A/B for every later compartment. Only one texture may occupy a compartment; cut it exactly at the next drum transient and never crossfade the change. Keep the environment 12 dB below the drums, place piano only after snares, and limit bass to one pitch per four bars. After 24 bars, stop the beat and expose one final empty compartment. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
