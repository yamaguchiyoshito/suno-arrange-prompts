```text
Create nocturnal post-dub around one fixed four-beat synth-bass phrase. Set a soft percussion loop in three beats, then extend its bar to four, five, six, and seven beats after eight cycles each. Add exactly one quiet hit at every new boundary. Never alter the bass notes, tempo, attack, or placement; let the changing bar move percussion against it. Add one original, hushed vocal sentence after the first alignment. Slice it into three fragments and return one only when both cycles meet, followed by short filtered echoes. Keep the mix dry and narrow during misalignment, then widen it briefly at shared downbeats. After seven beats, subtract the added hits in reverse while preserving the longer bars. End with bass alone after an incomplete echo. Avoid chord changes, fills, bright cymbals, drops, borrowed lyrics, and recognizable melodies.
```
