Create a sparse instrumental beat using MASS-HOLE's “bottle” only as a high-level structural reference. Record one original glass-bottle tap and transform it through a strict four-bar unit. Bar 1 contains one dry tap on beat 1. Bar 2 repeats that onset and adds exactly one echo three sixteenth notes later. Bar 3 removes the echo and instead excites one resonator a perfect fifth above the tap for two beats. Bar 4 contains no new attack; only the bar-3 resonator tail may remain. Repeat the unit three times, transposing the bottle trigger down one semitone per unit while the resonator stays a perfect fifth above it. A sub note may sound only on bar 1 beat 1; no snare, hi-hat, fill, or extra glass sound is allowed. End after the third tail reaches silence. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate MASS-HOLE or the source track.
