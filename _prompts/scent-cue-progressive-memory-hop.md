Create an 88 BPM instrumental mellow hip-hop piece with soft drums, upright-style bass, piano, and one original leaf-rustle recording used as a scent cue. Write a hidden eight-note piano melody and divide it into four consecutive two-note cells. At the first cue play only notes 1-2, at the second only 3-4, then 5-6, then 7-8. Do not sound those melody notes anywhere else. After the fourth cue, play the complete eight-note melody once in its original order and end. Keep the cue identical and unprocessed at every return. Compose and record everything from scratch; avoid recognizable melodies, samples, lyrics, or artist imitation.
