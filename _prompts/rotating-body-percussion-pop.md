```text
Create experimental body-percussion pop at 122 BPM. Write one twelve-hit accent cycle and divide its positions among handclaps, mouth clicks and breath pops, and low body-drum thumps. No two families may share a position. Every eight bars, rotate responsibility: claps move to mouth sounds, mouth sounds to low thumps, and low thumps to claps. Preserve every onset, rest, accent, and duration; change only timbre. Place a short original vocal hook only in the cycle’s rests and keep its melody and rhythm fixed. Complete three mappings, ending before the opening assignment returns. Avoid a drum kit, bass synth, fills, extra claps, tempo changes, crowd chants, and a large final chorus. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
