```text
Write warm midtempo indie rock at 84 BPM for lead singer, drums, bass, clean electric guitar, and organ. Keep every player at a constant dynamic and establish the complete ensemble before the coda.

Make the final 40 bars five consecutive eight-bar rooms. At bar 1 of the coda, the singer permanently exits. At bar 9, organ exits; at bar 17, guitar exits; at bar 25, drums exit. Bass performs the final eight bars alone and stops on a short note. An exiting part must end cleanly before its boundary, including delay and reverb, and may never return. Do not fade the master, automate remaining parts louder, add replacement layers, or increase anyone’s note density. The perceived reduction must come only from one performer leaving every eight bars. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
