```text
Create four original one-bar rhythmic phrases P1-P4 and four sharply distinct timbres: dry piano, muted guitar, vocal chop, and metallic percussion. Build four sixteen-bar sections. In section one assign the timbres to P1-P4 as A-B-C-D; then use B-C-D-A, C-D-A-B, and D-A-B-C. Repeat each assignment for four bars, so every phrase is rendered by every timbre exactly once across the piece. Preserve each phrase's onset, pitch contour, duration, velocity, and register whenever it returns. Keep one drum-and-bass loop and one original rap delivery unchanged through all sections. After section four, replay P1 alone in timbre A and stop. Do not layer two timbres on one phrase. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
