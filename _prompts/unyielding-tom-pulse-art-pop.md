```text
Create urgent art pop around one unyielding midtempo pulse. Anchor every section with a deep gated-tom pattern and low two-note synth throb. Keep both unchanged across verse and chorus; motion comes from persistence, not tempo or fills.

Set a direct lead vocal over wide, cold pads. Begin in a narrow register with little harmony. Each return adds one vocal element: low octave, distant answer, then sustained high voices. Use short guitar or synth accents only after phrases; the instrumental pulse remains identical.

Avoid four-on-the-floor kick, bright hats, funk bass, key changes, breakdowns, and EDM drops. Never mute the pulse. In the final section, overlap lead fragments and backing responses over the same tom figure, then remove all voices together. End with two bare repeats of tom and synth, without a resolving chord.
```
