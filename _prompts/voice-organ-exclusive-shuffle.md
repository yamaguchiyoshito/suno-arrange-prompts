```text
Write raw garage rock with a fixed 12/8 gospel shuffle, lead vocal, overdriven electric guitar, bass, drums, and compact organ. Enforce strict exclusion: whenever the singer sounds, the organ must be completely silent and guitar is the only harmonic support.

At every vocal rest, stop the guitar and let the organ answer for exactly the duration of the preceding sung phrase. The reply may transform that phrase's rhythm but must use a new melodic contour. Voice and organ may never overlap, including breaths, sustains, reverb, or pickup notes. Let bass and drums maintain the shuffle through every handoff. In the last exchange, withhold the expected organ reply and leave one empty phrase-length gap. Use wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, riff, vocal signature, recording, or sample.
```
