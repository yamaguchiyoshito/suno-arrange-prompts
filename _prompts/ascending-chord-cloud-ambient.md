```text
Create beatless ambient from attackless, choir-like pads, with no intelligible voice. Move each part at a different slow rate: one changes every 12 seconds, another every 18, and a high overtone every 30. Let overlaps form new chords without melody.

Begin near silence with one narrow midrange tone. Fade in other parts slowly, widening register and stereo space. Use smooth inversions, suspended intervals, and spectral shifts while hiding every onset.

Do not use drums, percussion, arpeggios, rhythmic gates, plucked sounds, or a defined bass line. Avoid cadences, hooks, and impacts. Create ascent through changing voicings, not pitch sweeps or rising volume. Near the end, remove the lowest layer and leave two upper tones hovering. Fade out before the next implied chord arrives.
```
