```text
Create instrumental drone-metal at 120 BPM from one sustained distortion mass split into three non-overlapping zones. A low kick ducks only 30–160 Hz, a tom only 160 Hz–2 kHz, and a metal strike only frequencies above 2 kHz. Use a fixed sparse pattern; no trigger may affect another zone. Every sixteen bars, remove one trigger permanently—high, then mid, then low—and fill only its exposed zone with a steady synthesized tone. Do not raise master level or add frequencies outside that zone. After all triggers vanish, hold the occupied spectrum for eight bars, then cut without decay. Keep pitch static. Avoid melody, chord changes, cymbal wash, fills, tempo ramps, stereo sweeps, and a drop. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
