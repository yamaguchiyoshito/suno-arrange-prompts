```text
Create a 69 BPM slow jam for four singers with clearly different registers, soft electric piano, rounded synth bass, restrained drums, and faint guitar haze. Assign each singer exactly eight bars as the sole lead; singers waiting for their turn must remain silent.

At each handoff, the outgoing singer introduces and sustains one new pitch. One beat later, the incoming singer joins that exact pitch; after one further beat the outgoing singer stops, and the new lead continues with an original phrase. This shared note is the only permitted vocal overlap. Choose a different handoff pitch each time and never repeat the singer order. After all four leads, let the instrumental continue without a final group chorus. Write wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, vocal arrangement signature, performance, recording, or sample.
```
