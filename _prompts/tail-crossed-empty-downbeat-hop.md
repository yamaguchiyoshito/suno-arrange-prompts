```text
Create an 86 BPM instrumental hip-hop track with drums, monophonic bass, and soft analog chords. In every bar, bass and chord attacks are forbidden on beat one. They must begin on the previous bar's beat four-and, sustain across the barline, and release before beat two; only the kick may attack on the downbeat. Do not add reverse swells, pickup notes at other positions, or replacement chord attacks. Keep this lifted-downbeat law through all sections and finish on a final downbeat containing kick plus inherited tails, never a fresh harmony. Use entirely original music and sound design; avoid existing samples or recognizable riffs.
```
