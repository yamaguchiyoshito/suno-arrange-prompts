```text
Create austere contemporary classical music at 48 BPM with no percussion. Make one original phrase from a bell-like piano triad, two bowed-string lines moving stepwise in contrary motion, and a low-string drone. Repeat it in four stages, doubling the full silence after each: one, two, four, then eight beats. At each stage remove one upper function and never restore it: all parts; remove the high moving string; remove the other moving string; keep only piano and drone. Fix triad voicing, intervals, dynamics, and room position. Let resonance fully decay in every silence; no entrance may anticipate. Avoid ornament, vibrato swell, ostinato, modulation, countermelody, crescendo, choir, and tonic emphasis. Do not return after the eight-beat silence. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
