```text
Create an original Japanese hip-hop track at 86 BPM with short low-register piano stabs, firm kicks, a dry cracking snare, restrained hats, and a compact bass line. Keep the groove mostly straight with small human timing differences. Put a clear, full-bodied rap lead close to the listener, using firm consonants, deliberate pauses, and controlled intensity. Treat the supplied lyrics as grounded street-level storytelling. Open with four bars of piano and drums, then alternate sixteen-bar verses and four-bar hooks. Keep the hooks rhythmically spoken, reinforced by one tight unison double. In the second verse, remove the piano for the first four bars so the voice and rhythm section carry the narrative. Restore the stabs and finish with a final hook, then cut on the last snare. Dense center, little reverb, strong words above the beat. Use original melodies, lyrics, vocal phrasing, and newly recorded material.
```
