```text
Create an intimate instrumental hip-hop piece using Nujabes' “Aruarian Dance” only as a high-level structural reference. Compose a new six-chord acoustic-guitar loop with a separate fixed bass note under each chord. Begin with every three-note upper voicing in close position. Every four bars transform exactly one chord, moving left to right through the loop: lift only that chord's lowest upper voice by one octave while its bass, other voices, onset, and duration remain unchanged. Previously transformed chords stay transformed. After all six are open, reverse the process from chord six back to chord one, lowering one upper voice per section. Keep the piano counterline and brushed beat identical throughout. Play the fully restored loop once, then stop after chord five so the expected sixth chord never arrives. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate Nujabes or the source track.
```
