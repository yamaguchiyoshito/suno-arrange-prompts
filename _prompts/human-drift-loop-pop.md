Loop-first groove with slight human drift. Drums are tight with a hint of swing; light kick, dry snare with short reverb.
Bass is root-led with passing tones; occasional octaves for lift.
Center on piano/keys; use add9/6/maj7 colors but avoid firm cadences.
Add EP/bell tones for sparse countermelody.
Vocal is intimate and dry; doubles very low.
Harmonies mainly 3rds, used sparingly. Preserve consonant clarity (careful 2–4 kHz EQ).
Structure: build from 8–16 bar loops, adding layers gradually.
Mix: tidy 200–2 kHz, restrained low end.
Space via plate + short room; don’t over-widen.
Keep emotion neutral—let repetition create trance.
