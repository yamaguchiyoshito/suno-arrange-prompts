```text
Create sparse instrumental minimal techno as a scan through three frequency zones. Keep a dry four-on-the-floor machine pulse and one original two-note synth-bass cell at a fixed tempo. Begin with sub frequencies alone: muted kick, deep sine bass, no top end. After eight bars, silence everything for one beat, then move the same rhythmic roles into a narrow middle register with clipped percussion and a hollow overtone. Repeat the cut and transfer them upward as ticks, hiss, and a thin pulse, leaving the bottom empty. Cycle through the zones again with small decay changes, never blending adjacent bands. Let all three sound together only once, for two bars near the end, then return to sub alone. Finish on a dry kick. Avoid chords, melody, vocals, risers, fills, distortion washes, and extra percussion.
```
