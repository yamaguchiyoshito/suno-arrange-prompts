```text
Build lean dub-funk at 88 BPM with conversational lead vocal, syncopated bass, dry drums, clipped guitar, compact organ, and one muted horn. Let the bass line contain deliberate rests measured in eighth notes, and make every rest choose its own response instrument.

For a one-eighth rest, guitar plays exactly one short chord. For a two-eighth rest, organ plays exactly one stab. For any rest lasting three or more eighths, the horn plays exactly one sustained note ending before bass returns. No other melodic instrument may sound inside that rest, and no reply may spill over the next bass attack. Construct an eight-bar bass phrase containing at least two examples of each rest class, repeat it three times, and rotate only the pitches of the replies. Leave the final four-eighth rest unanswered. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
