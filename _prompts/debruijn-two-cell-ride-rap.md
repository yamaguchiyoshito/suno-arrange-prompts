Arrange sixteen bars using the cyclic binary De Bruijn order AAAABAABBABABBBB, interpreted exactly as 0000100110101111 with A=0 and B=1. Loop the sixteen-bar route four times. Do not add a chorus, fill, transition sound, or third cell. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece.
