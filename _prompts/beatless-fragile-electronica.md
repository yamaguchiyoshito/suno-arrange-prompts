```text
Minimal electronic with warm, fragile textures.
Use soft sine-like synth tones, rounded FM keys, and tiny bell-like fragments.
Minimal or no kick. No snare. Low end: soft drone + restrained bass for float.

Harmony should feel floating and slightly unresolved, using suspended chords, maj7/add9 colors, and gentle modal movement.
Bass simple, often just a few rounded notes with long decay.

Let it sound naive, lonely, and transparent, almost like a toy instrument played through memory.
Use delay and reverb carefully, but keep the mix intimate.

Structure should not build like rock or EDM. Let it drift: motif → small variation → silence → gentle return.
The emotional goal is warm solitude.
```
