Create an original instrumental ambient piece without a fixed tempo or beat. Use bowed clean electric guitar for long, softly emerging tones and isolated upper harmonics, supported by a thin analog pad. Place a newly recorded wind-like room texture very low in the background. Keep the guitar free of distortion and sharp pick attacks. Begin with the air texture, let one low guitar tone bloom, then introduce higher harmonics one at a time as earlier tones decay. Allow overlapping notes to form open, gently changing chords without a repeating arpeggio. Keep the pad quieter than the guitar and let it support only a few common tones. Near the end, remove the pad and low guitar tone, leaving a final high harmonic to dissolve into the room texture. Intimate, weathered, luminous sound, with soft tape drift and generous space. Use original harmony, fresh performances, and newly made field recordings.
