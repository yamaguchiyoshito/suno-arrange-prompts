```text
Yoshinori Sunahara-style beatless arrangement

Make it almost motionless, dry, and highly controlled.
Remove drums completely.
No kick, no snare, no hats, no rhythmic pulse.

Use only a soft sub tone, thin analog pad, sparse electric piano, and tiny processed fragments.
Bass should not groove; let it appear as a low, sustained pressure, then disappear.

Harmony should be minimal: two or three clean chord tones, long gaps, no rich progression.

Melody should be fragmented and distant.
Use a single thin synth note, muted vibraphone tone, or chopped vocal sample as a small signal in space.

Mix direction: dry center, wide silent edges, controlled low end, clear high frequencies.
No warmth, no climax, no emotional performance.
Mood: empty airport terminal, glass architecture, midnight air conditioning.
```
