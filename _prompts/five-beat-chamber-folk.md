```text
Create intimate chamber folk in strict five-beat meter. Use fingerpicked nylon guitar as the clock, grouping each bar 3+2 without a heavy downbeat. Repeat it precisely; never drift into rubato or 4/4.

Place a close, restrained vocal above it. Let phrases cross bar lines while the guitar remains unchanged. Leave a breath after key lines. Add strings only in those gaps: cello answers first, then viola and violin add short counterlines. They should comment, not pad every chord.

Keep a dry room, audible fingers, soft bows, and modest dynamics. Avoid drums, bass, strummed choruses, runs, and constant string swells. Widen string intervals near the end but preserve the guitar figure and five-beat pulse. Finish with guitar alone after beat five.
```
