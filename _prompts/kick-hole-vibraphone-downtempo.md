```text
Make a warm 72 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop nocturne with a soft dusty break, round mono sub-bass, faint sustained strings, and a dry vibraphone surrounded by smoke-like room tone. Program a repeating two-bar drum grid with kicks on 1, the “and” of 2, 3, and the “and” of 4, but omit exactly one different kick on each repetition.

Place one short vibraphone note only in the vacated kick position; the vibraphone must never sound over an actual kick or anywhere else. Cycle the omitted position through all four choices, then repeat. Keep the bass to one pitch for each two-bar cycle and change the string chord only when the omission returns to beat 1. End after one final empty kick position with no vibraphone answer. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
