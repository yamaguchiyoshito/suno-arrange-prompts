```text
Piano is the spine—simple triads with added 9ths, wide voicings, lots of sustain.
Keep LH sparse to leave room for cello/contrabass. Vocal is intimate, almost dry; automate proximity (verses closer, chorus slightly wider).

Strings enter late and grow in tiers: solo cello → small ensemble → full section.
Write long legato lines; avoid busy counterpoint. Harmony moves slowly; delay cadences to heighten ache.

Drums are optional; if used, soft kick + brushed snare only at peak. Add subtle room noise/air for realism.

Form: long arc—whisper → swell → release. Automate dynamics more than arrangement.
Mix for depth: piano near, vocal center, strings back; hall reverb with longer pre-delay.
No hard compression—preserve transients and emotional rise.
```
