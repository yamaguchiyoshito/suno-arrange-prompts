```text
Create chamber pop from independent melodies, not block chords. Set warm bass and light percussion, then separate phrases for harpsichord, muted horn, low strings, and high bell. Keep distinct registers and answer across the bar.

Use a clear, tender lead. Verses allow only two counterlines. The chorus permits three overlapping lines without doubling the lead. Turn harmony through inversions while bass connects them; avoid key changes.

Avoid power chords, orchestral swells, solos, and dense pads. In the final section, stop adding instruments and turn the refrain into a round: one voice begins, a second enters later with the same contour, and a third answers lower. Remove percussion and instruments while voices continue. End on overlapping vocal lines and one soft horn note, not a full ensemble cadence.
```
