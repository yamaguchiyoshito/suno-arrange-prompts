```text
Yoshinori Sunahara-style minimal arrangement

Make it extremely restrained, dry, and precise.
Use only a short kick, tiny snare, closed hat, soft synth bass, and two or three clean chord tones.

Keep the drum pattern almost static.
No fills, no big drops, no emotional build.
Bass should play short notes, leaving more silence than sound.

Use thin analog pads or electric piano very sparingly.
Chords should appear like light reflections, not accompaniment.
Avoid thick harmony.

Melody should be fragmentary: a small synth phrase, one processed vocal cut, or a muted vibraphone tone.
Repeat it with almost no variation.

Mix everything clean and close.
Wide space, low volume movement, sharp edges, no dirt.
The mood is empty airport, silent city lights, luxury without warmth.
```
