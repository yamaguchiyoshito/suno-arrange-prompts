```text
Create vocal neo-soul at 86 BPM from four newly recorded two-second room-tone clips containing no speech or music. For each clip, measure its strongest stable resonance between 120 and 900 Hz and quantize that frequency to the nearest pitch class inside one chosen original key. If two clips produce the same pitch class, use the next-strongest unused resonance from the later clip. Those four results are the only pitch classes allowed in the electric-piano voicing; place one in each register from bass to soprano.

Whenever a chord tone sounds alone, play its originating room clip quietly beneath it; never attach a room clip to another tone. Keep a dry drum pocket, restrained synth bass, and close original rap-sung vocal around that harmonic object. Use only original melody, harmony, lyrics, performance, recordings, and sound design. Do not imitate DJ HASEBE, the referenced track, any recognizable vocal mannerism, riff, recording, or sample.
```
