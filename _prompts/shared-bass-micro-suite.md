```text
Create an instrumental abstract hip-hop micro-suite of four scenes, each twenty to thirty seconds. Bind them with one new three-note synth-bass cell; keep its pitch order, tone, and loudness while meter and harmony shift.

Scene one uses clipped found percussion and dry silence. Scene two cuts to harp droplets and tape haze. Scene three uses heavy asymmetric drums and granular sparks. Scene four removes drums for floating chords and room sounds. Join scenes with hard edits only—no crossfades, risers, fills, or transition effects. Start the bass cell on a different beat in each scene, but never alter its internal rhythm. End halfway through its last repetition. Create every source anew; never copy an existing melody, lyric, signature rhythm, sound recording, or recognizable sample.
```
