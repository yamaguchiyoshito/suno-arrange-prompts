```text
Create an anthemic indie-rock song with lead vocal, two electric guitars, bass, and drums all present from the first bar. Fix tempo, master loudness, rhythmic density, and player count for the entire piece; do not build by adding layers or playing harder.

Start every pitched part inside a narrow middle register. Every eight bars, move exactly one outer line up an octave and another outer line down an octave, keeping their rhythms and dynamics unchanged. Continue until guitars and vocal harmonies occupy extreme registers around the centered bass. At maximum span, collapse every part into the middle octave in one instant, then perform eight unchanged bars there. End without a volume climax. Compose wholly original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, anthem hook, guitar signature, performance, recording, or sample.
```
