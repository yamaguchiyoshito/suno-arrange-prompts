```text
Build a dusty 71 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop room piece with a worn two-bar break, a dry original two-note piano cell, low string stains, mono sub-bass, and distant unintelligible conversation ambience. Enforce strict bar alternation: in every odd-numbered bar the piano may sound and the sub-bass must be silent; in every even-numbered bar the sub-bass may sound and the piano must be silent.

Cut both instruments’ releases at each bar line so neither crosses into the other’s territory. Drums, strings, and room ambience may continue, but strings must hold one chord for eight bars and speech must remain nonverbal texture. After 32 bars, remove the drums and present one final odd/even exchange in the empty room. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
