```text
Build a two-part electronic-Americana suite around four distinct timbres: glassy synth, muted electric guitar, breathy low brass, and rounded synth bass. In a 45–60 second wordless prelude, introduce them one at a time in that reverse order: bass, brass, guitar, synth. Give each a new short gesture, then leave it faintly sustained.

At the first vocal entrance, reset to near silence and introduce the same timbres in forward order: synth, guitar, brass, bass. Do not reuse the prelude's melodies; let timbre order alone connect the chapters. Increase harmonic warmth as lower sounds arrive, but avoid a drum-driven drop. Once all four coexist, remove them in the prelude's order and leave the singer alone. Use wholly original music and words; never copy an existing melody, lyric, signature timbre chain, transition, recording, or sample.
```
