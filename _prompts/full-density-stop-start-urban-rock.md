```text
Create tense urban rock that begins at maximum density on the first downbeat, with no ambient intro or gradual build.

Drive tightly doubled electric guitars through hard chord stabs and clipped riffs. Add aggressive distorted organ in a separate midrange pocket. Keep the electric bass active with chord tones and chromatic approaches, not sustained roots.

Make live drums dry, close, and punchy: firm kick, cracking snare, controlled hi-hat, and little room sound. Use forceful straight eighth notes; avoid swing, loose pocket, and broad cymbal washes.

Build contrast with syncopated full-band stops. On offbeats, cut guitars, organ, bass, drums, lead, and backing voices to silence together. Return the band on one exact accent. Vary pause lengths without losing forward pressure.

Keep the lead direct and urgent above the dense band, with short dry ambience and sparse backing vocals. Finish on one synchronized hit, never a fade-out.
```
