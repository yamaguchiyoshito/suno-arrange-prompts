```text
Create slow psychedelic dream-pop at 72 BPM with airy lead vocal, soft electric piano, rounded bass, and barely touched drums. Base the accompaniment on one original four-beat cell and dilate only its note durations.

For bars 1–16, play eight eighth notes per cell. For bars 17–32, keep notes 1, 3, 5, and 7 and double them into four quarter notes. For bars 33–48, keep the first and third remaining notes and double them into two half notes. For bars 49–64, retain only the first pitch as one whole note. Each stage must remain exactly four beats long; do not raise tempo, loudness, or layer count. Let the vocal phrases lengthen in the same 1:2:4:8 proportion, using fresh words at every stage. End after one silent four-beat cell. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
