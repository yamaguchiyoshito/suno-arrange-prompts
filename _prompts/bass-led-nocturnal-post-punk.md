```text
Create nocturnal post-punk led by one melodic bass ostinato. Keep bass dry and forward, repeating the same narrow minor figure throughout. Lock restrained kick, snare, and closed hi-hat to a plain straight pulse with no swing or fills.

Add two clean guitars as distant signals: one plays clipped chord fragments, the other answers with long delay tails. Keep them above the bass register and leave silence after each phrase. Use a low, detached lead vocal with short lines. The chorus may widen the guitars but never replace the bass or brighten the harmony.

Avoid funk syncopation, power chords, lush pads, breaks, and triumphant release. Increase tension through longer repetition and slightly growing feedback. After the final line, continue the loop while removing guitar, then drums. Fade the repeating bass before it reaches a cadence.
```
