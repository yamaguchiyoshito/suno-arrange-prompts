```text
Create a 78 BPM vocal abstract hip-hop track in four eight-bar sections, A through D. Give each section an electric-piano harmony with one clearly different original root. Section A has no sub-bass. In B, hold only A's root as the sub; in C, hold only B's root; in D, hold only C's root. The sub must remain one complete section behind, with no passing tones, octave correction, slides, or anticipation. Keep one dry drum loop and spacious original rap delivery continuous. For the finale, remove electric piano and voice, then play delayed roots A, B, C, and D for two bars each over unchanged drums, finally resolving the missing D root. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
