```text
Create an instrumental musique-concrète suite at 116 BPM using new recordings from one household machine. Limit material to water intake, rotating motor, metal clicks, and warning chime. Follow one irreversible cycle: I, eight bars of pitched water pulses; II, twelve bars of motor-loop broken beat with metal clicks every fourth cycle; III, eight bars of descending drainage noise with no motor; IV, sixteen bars of accelerating spin texture while density stays fixed. Join sections by hard cuts in that order and never return. Derive bass, rhythm, and tone only by filtering or tuning those sounds. Avoid vocals, conventional instruments, borrowed recordings, risers, and chord progressions. After the motor stops, sound the chime twice, then leave silence. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
