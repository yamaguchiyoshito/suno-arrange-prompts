Create urgent vocal spiritual jazz at 88 BPM over one original ostinato for cello, upright bass, and dry handclaps. A close spoken-sung voice and rough muted trumpet compete for foreground and never lead together. Split three eight-bar sections into ownership: voice/trumpet 6:2 bars, then 4:4, then 2:6. The inactive lead stays fully silent. Keep ostinato pitches, tempo, and event count fixed while the balance reverses. Write concise original words about a current civic tension without slogans, quotes, names, or borrowed phrases. Trumpet answers the words' rhythm without copying their melody. Both leads meet only on the final beat in one new unison, then the ensemble cuts off. Avoid unassigned solos, chord changes, swing fills, choir, pads, and fade. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
