```text
Create an instrumental street-corner hip-hop piece using EVISBEATS' “Jaipur no Uraji nite” only as a high-level point of departure. Treat each bar as sixteen equal slots and allow featured attacks only on prime-numbered slots 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, and 13. Assign the six positions in repeating order to tabla, muted acoustic guitar, and breathy flute. Every four bars rotate all assignments forward by one instrument while keeping the six onsets, pitches, durations, and velocities unchanged. Pan successive prime attacks left, right, left, right, left, right; never automate movement between attacks. A quiet kick may mark slot 1, but no other percussion may enter. Complete exactly three rotations, then end with one empty bar containing only room tone. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate EVISBEATS or the source track.
```
