```text
Create sparse spoken dub at 138 BPM with a fixed half-time two-step pattern and original text. Give every line one stressed syllable. After an open “ah” or “oh,” answer with a descending sub-bass glide; after a narrow “ee” or “oo,” answer with an ascending glide. If a line ends on a hard consonant, replace the bass answer with one beat of silence. Never place bass beneath its trigger. Keep kick, rim, shuffled hat, tempo, and space unchanged. Across four verses, increase syllables per bar while preserving line length and drum speed, making speech alone create acceleration. Use brief echoes after bass answers, never on the lead voice. End on a consonant-triggered void. Avoid sung hooks, chord pads, risers, fills, and drops. Use only newly created material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyric, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
