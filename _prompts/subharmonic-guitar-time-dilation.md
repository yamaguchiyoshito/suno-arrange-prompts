```text
Create beatless instrumental drone metal with two low-tuned electric guitars and no percussion. Give guitar A a new two-note sonority and guitar B a different interval tuned a few cents away. Strike only one at a time; the other may enter only after the previous low fundamental has nearly vanished, making decay the clock. Sustain them through opposite amplifiers in a natural room. Keep picking force, gain, and loudness almost constant throughout. Develop the piece only by altering feedback angle, upper-harmonic emphasis, and each sonority's internal beating speed, never by adding notes or accelerating attacks. Sympathetic strings may bloom but cannot form a melody. Avoid drums, bass, vocals, riffs, chord progressions, crescendos, impact hits, and clean pads. End while an unstable interval still fades, before its expected answer.
```
