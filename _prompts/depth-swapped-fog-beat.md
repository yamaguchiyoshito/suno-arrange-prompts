```text
Create a hazy 70 BPM instrumental abstract hip-hop track with a dull laid-back break, blurred sub-bass, one glassy percussion fragment, and a low padded chord. Define two depth states: NEAR is dry with 8 ms pre-delay, 0.4 s decay, and open highs; FAR uses 70 ms pre-delay, 4 s decay, and a strong high-cut.

For bars 1–8, keep the glass fragment NEAR and the padded chord FAR. At every eight-bar boundary, swap only those depth settings between the two sounds. Do not change their notes, rhythm, volume, or stereo position. The drums and bass remain dry and fixed throughout, making the exchanged distance the only sectional change. Complete four swaps, then stop before a fifth. Compose wholly original music: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, distinctive riff, recording, or sample.
```
