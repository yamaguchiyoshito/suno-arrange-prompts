```text
Create instrumental jazz-fusion breaks around a new nine-note electric-bass phrase. Keep its pitch classes, accents, and rests unchanged. Program fast drums only inside those rests; no drum attack may double a bass onset.

Begin with kick and closed hat, then add snare ghosts, clipped rolls, and cymbal fragments without violating empty slots. Every sixteen bars, exchange register roles: transpose bass up an octave and thin its tone while drums shift toward low toms and sub kicks, then reverse next cycle. Build contrast through subtraction before addition, with no tempo changes. Place exposed bass cycles between dense passages. Finally remove drums in reverse entry order; stop the lone phrase before its last note. Use wholly original material; never copy an existing melody, lyric, signature bass line, drum break, recording, or sample.
```
