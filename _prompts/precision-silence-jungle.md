```text
Construct a precision jungle track from dry, high-speed drum fragments edited one hit at a time.
Use compact kick, hard snare, clipped rim, ghost notes, and metallic ticks. Reorder each break so accents answer across the stereo field; avoid preset loops.
Treat silence as percussion: cut tails, leave single-hit gaps, and insert empty half-bars that enlarge the next transient.
Bass: one deep mono sub with sparse pitch changes, marking structure rather than mirroring each kick.
Build 16 dense bars, then 4 bars of near-silence: remove the break, leave one ghost hit or sub pulse, and restart with a new hit order.
Keep harmony absent and melody minimal. No pads, lush reverb, vocal hook, or cinematic rise.
Mix surgical and dark, with sharply placed drums, short ambience, full transients, and black space around each important hit.
```
