Create nocturnal two-step with an incomplete shuffled pulse. Place kick on selected beats, leave downbeats empty, anchor with dry rim/snare plus loose sixteenth hats with drift and missing steps. Keep it human, unstable, quietly propulsive.

Add sparse root-note sub with long gaps, blurred minor chords, distant metal resonance, brief high haze. Avoid bright leads, clean piano, dense percussion, four-on-the-floor kick, and EDM drops. Turn anonymous vocals into pitched, stretched vowels, breaths, incomplete phrases. No recognizable quotation or continuous lyric; use dark reverb and short echoes at varied depths.

Weave quiet rain, wet steps, station ambience, and machinery into the rhythm; sometimes replace a drum hit with an environmental transient. Keep recordings sparse. Filter chords, drop kick for several bars, expose rain and voice, then restore part of the beat. End unresolved: sub-bass, one vocal fragment, and rain after the final hit.
