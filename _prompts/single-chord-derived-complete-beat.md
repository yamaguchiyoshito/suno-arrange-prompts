Record one original electric-piano chord once and use that single recording as the only instrumental source for an 86 BPM pop-rap track. Derive the kick from its first 80 milliseconds, pitched down two octaves and low-passed; derive the snare from its high-frequency residual after removing everything below 2 kHz; derive the bass from a looped fundamental; derive the pad from the reversed decay with its attack removed; derive a melodic stab by shortening the untouched chord to 120 milliseconds. Introduce these five derived roles one at a time, then combine them without any additional oscillator, drum sample, field recording, or instrument. Keep the vocal dry and rhythmically independent. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
