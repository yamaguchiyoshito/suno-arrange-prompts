```text
Write four original one-bar motifs A, B, C, and D in the same register for an 80 BPM rap track. Introduce A in section one, B in section two, C in section three, and D in section four; each section lasts eight bars. At every later section, transpose each already present motif downward by exactly one scale degree for every section it has lived through, while a newly introduced motif starts at its original register. Thus section four contains A down three degrees, B down two, C down one, and D unchanged. Continue four more sections with all motifs aging by one degree per section, then remove them oldest first. Keep rhythm, contour, timbre, dynamics, drums, and vocal meter fixed. Use only original performances, melodies, lyrics, samples, and recordings created for this piece. Do not imitate the source artist's melody, lyrics, vocal flow, signature rhythm, sound design, or recording.
```
