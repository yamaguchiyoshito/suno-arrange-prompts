Create instrumental sample-based hip-hop at 100 BPM using a four-by-four pad grid with rows A-D and columns 1-4. Load sixteen distinct newly recorded one-shots, one per pad. Trigger one pad on each eighth note across two bars, following this exact clockwise inward spiral: A1,B1,C1,D1,D2,D3,D4,C4,B4,A4,A3,A2,B2,C2,C3,B3. Every pad must sound exactly once per two-bar cycle, with no retrigger, overlap, flam, or empty slot.

Use drum and object hits on the outer twelve pads and four pitched FM fragments on the inner pads B2,C2,C3,B3. The spiral is the entire foreground beat; add only a sustained original sub tone beneath it. For the bridge, mute the outer pads while retaining their time slots as silence, then restore the unaltered route. Use only original composition, performances, recordings, and synthesis. Do not imitate Aru-2, Kzyboost, the referenced track, its groove, timbres, riffs, recording, or samples.
