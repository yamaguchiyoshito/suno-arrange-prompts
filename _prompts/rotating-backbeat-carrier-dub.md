```text
Create relaxed vocal rocksteady dub at 82 BPM with bass, minimal kick, snare, clipped guitar, organ, handclap, and easy lead voice. Beats 2 and 4 must be carried by exactly one timbre at a time.

Divide a 16-bar cycle into four four-bar blocks. In block one, only snare marks beats 2 and 4. In block two, mute snare there and let one guitar chop carry both beats. In block three, only a short organ stab carries them. In block four, only one dry handclap carries them. The three inactive carriers must be completely silent on those beats, including effects. Keep bass, kick, harmony, vocal density, and loudness unchanged across the rotation. Repeat once in reverse order, then leave the last expected beat 4 empty. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
