```text
Create kinetic new wave from interlocking loops, not chord changes. Anchor it with a single-note bass ostinato without fills on a firm four-beat pulse. Add clean, muted guitar in constant sixteenth notes, shifting accents instead of playing full chords.

Layer percussion in separate cycles: one part accents every three pulses and another every five, while drums hold the central meter. Introduce layers alone to reveal the composite groove before the full ensemble.

Begin with a dry, precise talk-sung lead in a narrow range. Turn repeated phrases into call-and-response, then a unison chorus with rough overlaps. Keep harmony static, the mix dry, and parts separated; build through phase, accent, and accumulation.

At the peak, remove guitar for one bar to expose bass and percussion cycles, then restore the group on one accent. After the chorus, cut voices and percussion together. End on isolated bass without a resolving chord.
```
