Create instrumental trance rock with two acoustic drum kits. Kit A holds plain 4/4 kick and snare; kit B repeats a five-accent tom-and-ride cycle across the same bar line. Keep both at one tempo and never let them copy each other's fills. Add a one-note electric-bass ostinato, then a clean delayed guitar pulse in a separate register. Write an original four-note electric-violin motif with long bows; repeat its pitch order while shifting its entry point every eight bars. Add one layer every sixteen bars. Keep all non-violin patterns stable after entry. At full density, let violin move up one octave while the rhythm section remains unchanged. Avoid verse-chorus form, chord changes, drum solos, rock breaks, and cinematic impacts. Subtract guitar, then violin, then the second kit, leaving bass and Kit A to stop together on beat one.
