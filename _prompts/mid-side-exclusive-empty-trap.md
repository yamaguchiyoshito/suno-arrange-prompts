```text
Create a hollow 70 BPM vocal trap track in repeating eight-bar blocks. Bars 1–6 are MID-only: mono kick, mono sub-bass, and one close original voice at center, with the M/S side channel at −∞ dB. Bars 7–8 are SIDE-only: one filtered analog pad and two object sounds, each copied to left and right with the right copy polarity-inverted; set the M/S mid channel to −∞ dB. Silence kick, sub, and voice, and remove all SIDE content below 120 Hz. Never overlap the two sets, including effects or transition smoothing. Keep both sets unchanged across four cycles and hard-cut every boundary. End after the final SIDE-only pair without returning to center. Use wholly original harmony, melody, lyrics, recordings, and samples; avoid recognizable riffs or vocal likenesses.
```
