```text
Create an original intimate ambient-pop song at 72 BPM. Use soft organ chords, a distant analog pad, a rounded low bass, and a barely audible brushed pulse. Keep a single unforced voice close and clear, singing short original lines in a comfortable range. Shape each four-bar phrase as two bars of singing followed by two bars without new vocal notes. Send only the final vowel of each line into a warm tape delay with two fading repeats; let those echoes occupy the vocal gap and disappear before the next line. Keep the organ and pulse steady beneath the pauses. Start with a four-bar instrumental intro, sing eight of these four-bar phrases, then use an eight-bar organ-and-pad outro. Let the last vocal echo vanish before the instruments fade. Gentle, reflective, quietly human, with restrained dynamics and no large chorus expansion. Compose fresh melody, harmony, lyrics, and vocal phrasing.
```
