```text
Create questioning vocal dub at 80 BPM with bass, drums, clean guitar, and low organ. Write each verse as four two-bar vocal questions, each ending before beat 4 of its second bar.

After every question, strike one snare on beat 4 and send it to a single one-beat delay. On beat 1 of the following bar, remove all direct sound: no kick, bass, guitar, organ, voice, room tone, or pickup. Only the delayed snare may land there. Resume the entire groove precisely on beat 2. Bars not following a question retain their normal downbeats. Repeat the four-question form twice, using a new melody and words on the second pass. After the last question, let the delayed snare reach the empty downbeat, but do not resume on beat 2; preserve the unanswered silence. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
