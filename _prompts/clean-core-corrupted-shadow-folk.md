```text
Create experimental glitch-folk around a dry, centered lead vocal and close acoustic guitar. Keep this core pair audible, mono-compatible, and completely unprocessed from beginning to end: no pitch correction, granular edits, reverse audio, or reverb on the core.

Duplicate only the guitar into left and right shadow layers. In successive sections add one irreversible fault to those shadows: slight pitch drift, then granular missing fragments, then short reversed decays. Never apply the same fault at the same moment on both sides. Keep the core rhythm and harmony stable while the shadows become less reliable. At the peak, mute both shadows for one exposed phrase, then return only their reversed tails. End on the intact central guitar. Write original music and lyrics; do not copy any existing melody, lyric, guitar signature, vocal style, recording, or sample.
```
