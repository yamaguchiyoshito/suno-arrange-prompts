```text
Create an instrumental abstract hip-hop beat using Nujabes' “Counting Stars” only as a high-level structural reference. Divide a repeating cycle into 24 equal sixteenth-note slots numbered 0–23. Place exactly six shared vibraphone-and-rim accents at 0, 1, 4, 10, 12, and 17; the fifteen pairwise distances between those marks must therefore occur without duplication. Leave slots 18–23 free of featured attacks. Repeat this timing map unchanged for twelve cycles while a newly composed chord changes every two cycles. Put kick only at slot 0 and bass only at slots 4 and 12; no fills, swing, added accents, or displaced echoes may alter the ruler. On cycle 12 remove the vibraphone but retain the rim positions, then stop at slot 18 instead of completing the silent tail. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate Nujabes or the source track.
```
