Create an original Japanese R&B-rap track at 100 BPM in 4/4 with a light two-step groove. Use bright FM bell plucks, smooth sliding sub bass, kicks on beat one and the offbeat after three, dry snare on two and four, shuffled hats, and soft chord pads. Write one short four-note motif. During sixteen-bar rap verses, let the bells play it in the gaps between vocal lines. During each eight-bar sung hook, mute the bells completely and give the same notes and rhythm to the singer, fitting the supplied lyrics naturally. Add quiet backing harmonies beneath the hook while keeping the lead clear. Use a four-bar intro, verse and hook, second verse and hook, then a four-bar instrumental outro where the bells reclaim the motif. Keep the pads and drum groove consistent through each handoff. Spacious, warm, lightly futuristic production with a solid low end and restrained vocal processing. Create original harmony, melody, vocal phrasing, and sound material.
