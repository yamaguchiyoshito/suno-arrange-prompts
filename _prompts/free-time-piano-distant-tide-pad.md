```text
Create an original instrumental ambient piece in free time, with no fixed BPM, drums, or regular pulse. Place a softly played acoustic piano close to the listener and a warm analog pad far behind it. Use short original melodic shapes, open chord voicings, and rests of unequal length. Keep the piano attacks audible while long, low-level reverberation carries their decay into the background. Let the pad hold common tones across the piano's changing harmony, moving slowly only when the old chord has nearly faded. Begin with piano alone, allow the pad to emerge gradually, then let it recede so the final phrase ends on exposed piano resonance. Maintain quiet dynamics and a small number of simultaneous notes. Warm, reflective, spacious, and gently luminous, with subtle tape softness rather than heavy noise. Compose fresh melodies and harmony and use newly performed material.
```
