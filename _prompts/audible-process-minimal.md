```text
Create instrumental process minimalism through repetition and note-level change. Begin with an eight-note pattern shared by dry piano and soft vibraphone. Keep harmony modal and consonant; avoid verse-chorus form.

Every four bars, change exactly one note: fill an empty subdivision or remove one note. Never do both together, alter two pitches, transpose the pattern, or add a new progression. Mark each bar with muted low piano. Once established, add a faint dry shaker to clarify subdivisions, never a groove. Repeat precisely with natural accents.

Favor additions in the first third, alternate changes in the middle, then remove one note every four bars until two distant tones remain. Keep dynamics narrow. No drops, risers, fills, key changes, dramatic crescendos, or chorus. Mix with short room ambience, distinct attacks, and stereo separation so layers stay traceable. End after the final two-note pattern repeats for four bars.
```
