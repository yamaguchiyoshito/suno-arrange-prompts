```text
Write luminous psychedelic pop at 96 BPM with lead vocal, bass, drums, clean guitar, and organ. Harmony may change only when a delayed vocal ending authorizes it.

Send only the final pitched syllable of each vocal line to a single dotted-quarter-note delay with one repeat. If that repeat crosses the next barline, change to a new chord on the immediately following downbeat. If it dies before the barline, hold the current chord for another full bar. Guitar, organ, and bass must change together; no anticipations or passing chords may precede the authorized downbeat. Use vocal lines ending at varied positions so the rule produces both held and changing bars. In the final refrain, omit the delay from the last syllable and remain on the unresolved chord for four bars. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
