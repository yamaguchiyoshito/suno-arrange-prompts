Create a qawwali-inspired arrangement from original lyrics and melodies with lead voice, response chorus, harmonium, tabla, dholak, and handclaps. Begin with a free-time solo over one sustained harmonium tone. Establish a medium pulse with paired claps, then add interlocking tabla and dholak instead of a backbeat. The lead sings a new phrase; chorus repeats only its final words. Shorten exchanges from four bars to two, then one, raising vocal density while tempo increases slightly. Let harmonium shadow the lead at first, then hold a drone as voices intensify. Insert one percussion-only cycle before the final return. End with three unison responses and one collective cutoff. Avoid synth bass, pads, Western chord changes, drum fills, and imitation of any singer.
