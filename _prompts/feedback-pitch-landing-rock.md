```text
Create floating vocal post-rock at 68 BPM with sustained guitar feedback, bass, restrained drums, organ, and distant lead voice. Before every chord change, assign the guitar a feedback pitch that must bend continuously toward the third of the next chord.

Alternate bend journeys of exactly two, three, and five beats. During each journey, voice, bass, organ, and drums must hold the current harmony without anticipations. Only after feedback reaches the target third may the full band change chord together on the next beat. If the pitch overshoots, hold the old chord until it returns accurately. Use six transitions, repeating the 2–3–5 duration cycle twice, and increase intensity only by widening the feedback tone, never by adding notes. For the ending, let the final bend stop just below its target and deny the last chord change. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
