```text
Create an instrumental dub piece using 16FLIP's “Byrd Dub” only as a high-level point of departure. Divide each bar into sixteen slots and place short original FM chirps only on prime-numbered slots 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11. For every chirp, generate exactly three separated repeats, producing a four-event flight path at pitch offsets 0, +2, +5, and +9 semitones. Pan those four events hard left, center, hard right, then center; use no continuous panning. Cut feedback after the third repeat and allow chains to cross barlines without quantizing them. Keep a low kick at slots 1 and 9 and a rim at slot 13, unchanged for eight bars. On bar 9 omit all source chirps but let only previously launched repeats finish, then end when the last center echo stops. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate 16FLIP or the source track.
```
