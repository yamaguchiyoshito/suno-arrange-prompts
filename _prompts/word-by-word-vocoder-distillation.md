```text
Compose an unaccompanied vocal piece from one newly written six-word sentence. Present it once in a single dry voice. Repeat the sentence six times; on each pass remove exactly one additional word and convert that word into a sustained, harmonized processed-vocal pitch beginning at its former rhythmic position.

Keep every converted pitch sounding through the remaining repetitions. Never restore a removed word, change its assigned pitch, or add instruments and drums. Choose the six pitches so the accumulating sonority moves from open consonance to a close, unresolved cluster. On the final pass no sentence remains: only six sustained vocal tones. Let breaths and tuning imperfections remain audible, then cut all tones together. Use wholly original words, melody, harmony, and sound design; never reproduce an existing lyric, melodic phrase, signature vocal effect, recording, or sample.
```
