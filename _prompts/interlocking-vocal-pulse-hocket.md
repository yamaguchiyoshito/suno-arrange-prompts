```text
Create experimental vocal pop at 105 BPM in 4/4. On eighth notes, Voice A repeats five pulses and Voice B seven, using original nonsense syllables. Make a strict hocket: they never attack together; at collisions, alternate which voice yields. Let the resulting gaps drift audibly across bar lines. A soft synth bass plays one short note only when both voices rest. Keep two chords, and bar instrumental attacks from vocal positions. Every eight bars, swap the voices' stereo positions and registers without altering either cycle. At bar 16, mute the bass for one combined cycle, then restore it quietly. Avoid chorus form, drums, unisons, lyrics, and cadential resolution. End midway through the seven-pulse cycle. Use only newly created original material; do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, recording, or sample.
```
