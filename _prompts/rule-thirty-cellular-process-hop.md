Create a 96 BPM instrumental process hip-hop track on a sixteen-step grid. Keep kick on beats 1 and 3 and snare on 2 and 4. For one bright percussion sound, begin with the binary row 1000000000000000. Generate each following bar with cellular automaton Rule 30: a new cell equals left XOR (center OR right), with the two ends wrapped together. Sound only cells equal to 1. Continue for sixteen generated bars, then return once to the seed and stop. Do not add fills, humanization, or another evolving sequence. Use original sounds and harmony only; do not reproduce any existing melody, sample, or recording.
