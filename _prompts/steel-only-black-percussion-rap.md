Create an 82 BPM original vocal hip-hop track using percussion recorded from exactly six steel objects: a plate, pipe, chain, tool, can, and spring. Assign one object to each rhythmic role and never layer conventional kick, snare, clap, cymbal, or wood hit beneath it. Add only a dry monophonic bass and an original rap vocal. Preserve a short identifiable metallic resonance after every strike; do not flatten the objects into generic drum samples. Leave one full beat empty after every eight-bar verse. All lyrics, rhythms, pitches, performances, and recordings must be original; avoid recognizable samples, flows, or artist likeness.
