```text
Create an 87 BPM instrumental hip-hop ensemble from exactly six original household recordings: glass, paper, key, drawer, ceramic bowl, and wooden box. Tune each source by no more than 50 cents to one fixed pitch of a six-note scale, then lock it there for the entire track. A source may never play a second pitch, and no synthesizer or conventional drum sample may be added. Preserve each object's natural duration and build harmony through combinations of objects rather than repitching. Use an original scale, rhythm, and recordings only; do not imitate or sample any existing music.
```
