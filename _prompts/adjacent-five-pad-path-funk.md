Create a 108 BPM instrumental electro-funk beat with five pads arranged in a ring: kick, hat, chord, snare, bass. Perform a continuous one-hand pattern in which every new hit must move to the immediately clockwise or counterclockwise neighboring pad. Repeating the same pad or jumping across the ring is forbidden. Record a sixteen-bar unquantized take, then loop it exactly once with no edits, overdubs, fills, or added notes. Let velocity remain human while the adjacency rule shapes the groove. Use only original drum sounds, bass, harmony, and recordings; do not copy a known pattern or sample.
