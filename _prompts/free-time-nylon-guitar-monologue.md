```text
Create an intimate solo with nylon-string guitar and one close-miked vocal. Use free time without a click, fixed meter, repeating groove, or countable pulse. Shape phrases around each breath, leaving silence before key words and chord changes.

Fingerpick softly with fingertips. Use asymmetrical isolated bass notes, incomplete arpeggios, open strings, and occasional harmonics. Retain slides, fret noise, nail contact, and faint body creaks without making them percussion.

Keep the voice fragile and conversational, from whisper to clear tone without belting. Let voice and guitar follow loosely; either may hesitate or lead. Add no drums, percussion, bass, piano, pads, strings, backing vocals, or electronics.

Record in a small quiet room with short ambience and wide dynamics. Avoid quantization, tempo correction, heavy compression, and stereo widening. End on an open unresolved voicing; let the final resonance and breath decay naturally.
```
