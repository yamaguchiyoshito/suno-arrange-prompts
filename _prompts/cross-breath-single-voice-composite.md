```text
Create a free-time, single-voice electro-acoustic song. Record one original melody twice as complete takes A and B in the same room, without a click. For every lyric line, compare the audible inhalations before it. Keep the longer inhalation, then hard-splice it at the vocal onset to the sung body from the opposite take: breath A must lead to line B, or breath B to line A. Only one vocal signal may sound at any instant. Do not overlap, double, harmonize, crossfade, time-align, tune, or stretch either take, and never use both sung bodies for one line. Support the composite with isolated muted-piano tones and a continuous low room tone; use no drums or pulse. Leave every selected breath fully audible and finish on unprocessed room decay. Use wholly original melody, lyrics, performance, recordings, and sounds; do not imitate any identifiable song or vocal likeness.
```
