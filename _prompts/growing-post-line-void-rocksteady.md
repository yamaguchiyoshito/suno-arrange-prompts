```text
Write tender vocal rocksteady dub at 78 BPM with clean offbeat guitar, deep bass, light drums, and an exposed lead voice. After each two-bar sung line, stop the entire ensemble and all effect returns, then resume the same groove at unchanged volume.

Use four lines per verse. After line one, leave exactly one quarter note of total silence. After line two, leave one half note. After line three, leave one full 4/4 bar. After line four, leave two full bars. Repeat the verse with new lyrics but preserve the same silence ladder. Do not fill the gaps with breaths, pickups, delay, room tone, or count-ins. In the final cycle, sing only the first three lines, reach the one-bar silence, and never return for line four. Make the result wholly original: do not reuse any existing melody, lyrics, signature riff, vocal imitation, recording, or sample.
```
