Create vocal city G-funk at 100 BPM from four newly recorded one-shots labeled North, East, South, and West. Divide every bar into sixteen steps. Program only North at step 0. North must trigger East three steps later at step 3; East triggers South five steps later at step 8; South triggers West four steps later at step 12; West sends a four-step return pulse that triggers the next bar's North at step 0. East, South, and West may never receive independent sequencer notes.

Keep this 3-5-4-4 trigger ring beneath original synth bass, drum-machine backbeat, melodic whistle, and four contrasting original rap voices. At bar 17, suppress one North trigger so the entire found-sound ring disappears for exactly one bar, then manually restart North at bar 18. Use only original melody, harmony, lyrics, performances, field recordings, and synthesis. Do not imitate DJ PMX, the referenced track, its rappers, recognizable flows, riffs, recording, or samples.
