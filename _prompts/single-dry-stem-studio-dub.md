```text
Create an instrumental dub beat using 16FLIP's “16Studio Dub” only as a high-level structural reference. Use four original sources: drums, sub bass, one-key sampler stabs, and a newly recorded room texture. In every four-bar block, exactly one source may be heard dry; mute the other three dry channels and hear them only through a shared tape delay and spring-style reverb return. Rotate dry ownership in this fixed order: drums, bass, stabs, room, then repeat once. Do not automate send levels inside a block, and never let two sources become dry together. Notes, onsets, velocities, feedback, and return levels remain identical while ownership changes. In the final block assign dry status to the room again, but stop all three wet musical sources after beat three and leave one bar of dry room. Use original material, melodies, lyrics, and recordings only; never imitate 16FLIP or the source track.
```
